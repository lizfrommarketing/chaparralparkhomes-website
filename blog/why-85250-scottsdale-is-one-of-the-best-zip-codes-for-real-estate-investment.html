Why 85250 Scottsdale Is One of the Best Zip Codes for Real Estate Investment

Tenant stability, rental demand, appreciation and what to stress-test before you buy — the case for 85250 from a resident agent.

I get asked about investment properties in the Phoenix metro regularly. Investors call me from California, Chicago, and the Northeast — often with a list of zip codes they've pulled from some national ranking. My answer is always to look closer at 85250 first.

Not because it's my backyard. Because the fundamentals are genuinely strong — and I can show you exactly why.

What Makes a Zip Code a Good Investment?

Before I make the case for 85250, let's establish what smart real estate investors actually care about:

  • Tenant quality and stability — Are renters staying put, or is turnover constant?
  • Rental demand — Is there a reliable pool of tenants who want to live here?
  • Appreciation — Are home values moving up over time, or stagnant?
  • Vacancy risk — How fast do properties lease up when they go vacant?
  • Resale liquidity — When you're ready to sell, are buyers waiting?

The 85250 zip code scores well on every single one of these. Here's why.

Tenant Stability: Families and Professionals Don't Move Often

The Chaparral Park neighborhood and broader 85250 area attract a specific tenant profile: families zoned into Scottsdale Unified School District, healthcare professionals working near the Scottsdale healthcare corridor, and professionals who want walkable access to Old Town Scottsdale and Scottsdale Fashion Square.

These aren't transient renters. Families with kids in SUSD schools don't move every 12 months — disrupting their children's education is a major deterrent. Professionals who have settled into the Old Town lifestyle aren't easily poached by cheaper zip codes further out. Tenant stability in 85250 is structurally higher than most of the Phoenix metro.

Rental Demand: 85250 Has a Built-In Waitlist Mentality

Scottsdale is one of the top relocation destinations in the entire United States. Remote workers, retirees, healthcare professionals, and families leaving high-tax states like California, Illinois, and New York are landing in Scottsdale in significant numbers.

When those buyers and renters start their search, 85250 is consistently on the shortlist because of what it delivers: top schools, walkable lifestyle, proximity to Old Town, and a safer neighborhood profile than most of the Valley. That built-in demand keeps vacancy rates low and gives landlords leverage on pricing.

Appreciation: Central Scottsdale Holds Value

The 85250 zip code benefits from what real estate investors call a “central location premium.” Unlike suburban Scottsdale zip codes further north, 85250 is landlocked by quality — surrounded by established neighborhoods, city infrastructure, and commercial amenities that don't get displaced by new development.

New inventory can't suddenly appear and depress values the way it can in outer-ring suburbs. The parks, the school district, the proximity to Old Town and Fashion Square — these don't change. That scarcity is baked into the long-term appreciation story.

The Short-Term Rental Angle

Scottsdale is one of the top short-term rental markets in the country, driven by its status as a premier destination for corporate events, spring training baseball, golf tourism, bachelorette and bachelor parties, and snowbirds from colder states.

The 85250 zip code's proximity to Old Town Scottsdale and Fashion Square makes it a strong location for short-term rental investors as well as traditional long-term landlords. Before going this route, always verify current city of Scottsdale short-term rental regulations — the rules have evolved and you want to be fully compliant from day one.

What an Investment Budget Buys in 85250

Most 85250 homes sell between roughly $420K and $775K (median about $600K–$650K, as of October 2026). In that range, investment buyers are typically looking at single-family homes with strong rental histories, townhomes in walkable locations near the greenbelt or Fashion Square, and properties with renovation upside that can be repositioned for higher rents or resale.

The key metrics to run: purchase price vs. current market rent, estimated cap rate, and projected appreciation over your intended hold period. I work with investors on exactly this analysis — not just finding the property but stress-testing the numbers before you commit.

Why Work With a Local Expert for 85250 Investment Properties?

Off-market opportunities, accurate rental comps, and neighborhood-specific knowledge matter more in a competitive zip code like 85250 than in a slower market. I live here. I know which streets command premiums, which HOA situations to avoid, and which properties are quietly coming to market before they hit the MLS.

If you're evaluating 85250 for an investment purchase, let's talk numbers before you start submitting offers.
