Old Town Scottsdale Farmers Market 2026-27: New Location, Hours and Parking

The Old Town Scottsdale Farmers Market moved to the City Hall lot at 3939 N. Drinkwater Blvd. Saturdays 8 a.m.–1 p.m. through April. Hours, parking and tips.

Where is the Old Town Scottsdale Farmers Market now? It moved. For the 2026-27 season, the market is at the City Hall surface lot, 3939 N. Drinkwater Blvd., in the Civic Center. It's open Saturdays, 8 a.m. to 1 p.m., October through April. The longtime lot at First and Brown Avenue is now a construction site for a new parking garage.

Is it the same market? Yes, with more room. The City of Scottsdale called the City Hall lot a "new, larger location" that keeps the market close to Old Town shops and restaurants. Expect more than 100 local growers and specialty food producers, from produce and eggs to breads, jams and tamales.

Data note: Dates, hours and location come from Old Town Scottsdale's official market page and the market organizer, Arizona Community Farmers Markets. Relocation details come from the Scottsdale Progress (Oct. 10, 2026, updated Oct. 19). Vendor attendance varies week to week. No market statistics are included in this post.

Old Town Scottsdale Farmers Market at a Glance

  • Address: City Hall lot, 3939 N. Drinkwater Blvd., Scottsdale, AZ 85251
  • Day: Saturdays only
  • October–April: 8 a.m.–1 p.m.
  • May: 8 a.m.–noon
  • June: 7–10 a.m.
  • Closed: July, August and September
  • Season: opened Oct. 3, 2026, runs through June 2027
  • Vendors: 100+ local growers and specialty food producers
  • Payment programs: organizer says SNAP and Double Up Food Bucks are accepted, and the market is enrolled in the AZFMNP/WIC program

Why the Market Moved

From the Parking Corral to Civic Center

The market spent years at the First and Brown "Parking Corral." According to the Scottsdale Progress, that lot is fenced off for a new multi-level garage, so the market moved a few blocks to City Hall.

What's New at City Hall

  • Two new solar shade canopies over the lot, per the city
  • New electrical access for the market and other community events, part of a voter-approved bond project
  • Old Town ambassadors helped guide shoppers from Brown Avenue during the first month

Parking for the Farmers Market

The organizer lists three options:

  • 7469 E. Indian School Road
  • Benny Gonzalez Way, between Drinkwater Boulevard and 75th Street
  • Civic Center Library parking structure, 3803 N. Drinkwater Blvd.

The Scottsdale Progress also notes parking directly across the street at the One Civic Center surface lot. On busy Saturdays, the library garage is a dependable fallback.

What You'll Find

Farmers and Ranchers

  • Locally grown, often organic produce
  • Free-range eggs and local meats
  • Citrus, microgreens and cut flowers in season

Prepared and Specialty Foods

  • Artisan breads and pastries
  • Cheese, jams, hummus and hot sauces
  • Tamales and global dishes
  • Coffee, chai, kombucha and fresh juices

The organizer describes this as a food and farming market, so it isn't a crafts fair.

Upcoming Classes

The organizer lists a monthly wild-foods class at the Original Arizona booth, 9–11 a.m.:

  • Nov. 21, 2026: Preserving Wild Foods
  • Dec. 12, 2026: Legal Foraging in Arizona
  • Jan. 16, 2027: Culinary Ashes and Binders

Tips for a Smooth Saturday

  • Go early for the best produce selection
  • Bring reusable bags and a small cooler for meat, eggs and cheese
  • Leashed, well-mannered pets are welcome, according to the organizer
  • Pair it with the greenbelt: a morning walk on the Indian Bend Wash path before shopping makes a great Saturday. See the Indian Bend Wash Greenbelt
  • Stay for lunch in Old Town. See living minutes from Old Town Scottsdale

A Short Drive From Chaparral Park

From the Chaparral Park area, Civic Center is a short drive south. The new location sits right off Drinkwater, so you skip the Brown Avenue side streets entirely. More weekend ideas: fall weekends near Chaparral Park.
