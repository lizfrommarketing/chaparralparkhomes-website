How to Choose a Realtor to Sell Your House: 10 Questions to Ask Before You List in 85250

Choosing a listing agent in 85250? The 10 questions to ask, how to verify an Arizona license, and why October 2026's 89-day market time makes pricing and marketing plans matter.

How do you choose a realtor to sell your house? Interview two or three agents who actively sell in your area, and choose the one whose price is backed by recent comparable sales and whose marketing plan is specific, written and realistic. Then confirm the license in the Arizona Department of Real Estate public database before you sign.

Why it matters right now in 85250: Single-family homes that sold in October 2026 took a median 89 days from listing to sale, and sellers received 96.8% of list price, according to Phoenix REALTORS. In a market that moves at that pace, pricing and presentation decide the outcome more than they did in a faster year.

Data note: Market figures are from the Phoenix REALTORS/ShowingTime 85250 Local Market Update for October 2026 (ARMLS data, current as of November 3, 2026). License lookup information is from the Arizona Department of Real Estate website. Read November 23, 2026.

What the 85250 Market Asks of a Listing Agent

Single-family, October 2026

  • Median sale price: $825,000 (year-to-date median $865,000, up 6.8%)
  • Days on market until sale: 89, up from 80 a year earlier
  • Months of supply: 4.2, with 51 homes for sale
  • New listings: 15, versus 27 in October 2025

Townhouse/condo, October 2026

  • Median sale price: $475,000
  • Inventory: 113 homes, up 22.8%, for 5.7 months of supply

A home that sits for three months has to stay fresh in buyers' minds. That's why the questions below focus on pricing logic, marketing and communication.

The 10 Questions to Ask Every Agent

About pricing

  1. "Which recent sales did you use, and why?" Ask for the comparable sales in writing, with adjustments for size, condition, lot and updates
  2. "What happens if we get no offers in the first few weeks?" A good agent has a plan for reviewing feedback and price before you lose momentum
  3. "What should I fix, and what should I leave alone?" The best answers save you money as often as they spend it

About marketing

  1. "Show me your marketing plan." Photography, video, floor plans, online placement and showings, with dates
  2. "How will you describe my home?" Listen for real features: lot, updates, location near the Indian Bend Wash greenbelt or Old Town
  3. "How do you handle showings and feedback?" You should get feedback after showings, not just a count

About experience and fit

  1. "What have you sold nearby recently?" Local sales mean local buyer insight
  2. "Who will I actually work with?" Know whether it's the agent, a team member or an assistant day to day
  3. "How often will we talk, and how?" Agree on a rhythm: weekly calls, texts after showings, whatever suits you

About the agreement

  1. "What are the listing term, fees and cancellation terms?" Read the listing agreement before you sign, and ask about anything you don't understand. Fees are negotiable between you and your agent

How to Verify an Arizona Real Estate License

The Arizona Department of Real Estate (ADRE) offers a free Public Database at azre.gov. You can search for licensees, real estate entities and disciplinary actions posted by the Department. It takes two minutes and should be part of every interview.

Red Flags Worth Noticing

  • A price far above the comps with no clear explanation. It can win the listing and then cost you weeks on the market
  • A vague marketing plan such as "it goes on the MLS and the big sites"
  • Pressure to sign today
  • No written analysis to take home and compare

Comparing Agents Side by Side

After your interviews, line up each agent's suggested list price, the comps behind it, the marketing plan and the listing terms. The right choice is rarely the highest price or the lowest fee. It's the agent whose plan you understand and trust.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose a realtor to sell my house?

Interview two or three local agents, compare their written pricing and marketing plans, and verify the license with ADRE.

What questions should I ask a realtor before selling my home?

Ask about comps, marketing, communication, what to fix, and the listing terms and fees.

How do I check if a real estate agent is licensed in Arizona?

Use the Public Database on the Arizona Department of Real Estate website.

Should I pick the agent who suggests the highest price?

Not on that basis alone. Choose the price that is backed by recent comparable sales.
