Should I Buy a House Now or Wait Until 2027? What Scottsdale's October 2026 Numbers Say for 85250 Buyers

Scottsdale buyers have 1,480 homes to choose from and pay 96.8% of list on average, but 30-year rates are above 7%. How to decide whether to buy now or wait in 85250.

Should you buy a house now or wait? If you have a stable income, plan to stay at least several years and the monthly payment works at today's rates, Scottsdale's current market gives you more choice and more negotiating room than buyers have had in years. Phoenix REALTORS' October 2026 report shows 1,480 single-family homes for sale in Scottsdale, 4.2 months of supply, and buyers paying 96.8% of list price on average.

The trade-off is the rate. Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed average was 7.18% on Nov. 5, 2026, up from 6.22% a year earlier. Waiting could bring a lower rate, or it could bring higher prices and fewer listings. Nobody can promise which. Here is how to think it through for 85250.

Data note: Market figures are from the Phoenix REALTORS/ShowingTime Local Market Update for Scottsdale, October 2026 (current as of Nov. 3, 2026; ARMLS data). Mortgage rates are from Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey for the week of Nov. 5, 2026. Payment examples are simple arithmetic, not loan quotes. Read November 12, 2026.

Scottsdale Buyer Conditions at a Glance (October 2026)

Single-family homes:

  • Median sales price: $1,225,000 (up 5.6% from $1,160,000)
  • Homes for sale: 1,480 (flat year over year)
  • Months supply of inventory: 4.2 (down from 4.4)
  • Days on market until sale: 75
  • Percent of list price received: 96.8%
  • Pending sales: 208, down 43.5% from October 2025

Townhouses and condos:

  • Median sales price: $485,000 (up 4.3%)
  • Months supply of inventory: 6.6 (up from 5.5)
  • Homes for sale: 1,127 (up 12.0%)

What it means: prices are still higher than a year ago, but buyers are taking their time and sellers are negotiating. Condo buyers have the most leverage right now.

The Case for Buying Now

More homes to compare

With nearly 1,500 single-family listings and 6.6 months of condo supply, you can tour, compare and walk away when a home isn't right. That's a very different experience than a market where homes go under contract in a weekend.

Room to negotiate

At 96.8% of list price, the average Scottsdale single-family buyer paid about 3.2% below the last asking price. On a home listed at the median of $1,225,000, that gap works out to about $39,200 (illustrative arithmetic). Sellers are also offering credits; see our guide to seller concessions and rate buydowns.

Less competition during the holidays

Pending sales fell 43.5% from a year earlier in October. Fewer active buyers usually means fewer multiple-offer situations, especially from Thanksgiving through New Year's.

The Case for Waiting

Rates are higher than last year

Here is what the rate difference means on a $980,000 loan (20% down on a $1,225,000 home), principal and interest only:

  • At 7.18%: about $6,639 per month
  • At 6.68%: about $6,311 per month
  • At 6.22% (last year's level): about $6,015 per month

Half a point is worth about $328 a month on that loan. That's real money, but it is not a guarantee: rates can rise as easily as they fall.

Your life isn't ready yet

If your job, family plans or savings are in flux, waiting is the right call regardless of the market. Closing costs and moving costs make a short ownership period expensive. Our closing cost guide for Arizona buyers breaks them down.

A Simple Decision Test for 85250 Buyers

Ask yourself four questions:

  • Can I afford the payment at today's rate without counting on a refinance?
  • Will I stay at least five years or so?
  • Do I have reserves after the down payment and closing costs?
  • Have I found the right home, or am I buying because of a headline?

If the answers are yes, buying now lets you negotiate in a slower market. If the answers are no, waiting costs you nothing but time. Seasonality matters too: our guide to the best time to buy a house in Scottsdale covers how the calendar shifts leverage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I buy a house now or wait until 2027?

Decide on your timeline and payment comfort, not predictions. Buyers have 4.2 months of supply and 96.8% list-to-sale pricing, with rates at 7.18%.

Is it a good time to buy a house in Scottsdale right now?

Buyers have more leverage than in recent years: 75 days on market and 96.8% of list price in October 2026.

Should I wait for interest rates to drop before buying a house?

Only if today's payment doesn't work for you. Refinancing later is possible but not guaranteed, and prices can rise while you wait.

Are Scottsdale home prices dropping?

Not year over year: the single-family median was $1,225,000 in October 2026, up 5.6%.
