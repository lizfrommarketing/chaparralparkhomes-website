North Scottsdale vs. South Scottsdale: Prices, Pace and Lifestyle Compared by ZIP Code

North Scottsdale vs. south Scottsdale, ZIP by ZIP: 2026 median prices from $635,500 to $1.73 million, months of supply, and the Preserve-versus-Greenbelt lifestyle split.

North Scottsdale or south Scottsdale: which is right for you? North Scottsdale generally means newer homes, bigger lots and Preserve trailheads at a higher price, with 2026 single-family medians from about $1.08 million to $1.73 million. South and central Scottsdale means walkable access to Old Town and the Indian Bend Wash Greenbelt, with medians from about $636,000 to $918,000.

The pace differs too. In October 2026, months of supply ranged from 3.5 in 85260 to 7.1 in 85262, so "Scottsdale market conditions" depend heavily on which ZIP you're shopping.

Data note: Prices, supply and days on market are from Phoenix REALTORS Local Market Updates for each ZIP, October 2026 (single-family). Year-to-date medians run January through October 2026 and are steadier than single-month figures in small ZIPs. Read November 18, 2026.

How I'm Defining North and South

There's no official dividing line, so here's the framing I use with clients:

  • South and central: 85257 (south of Old Town, near Tempe), 85251 (Old Town and surroundings), 85250 (Chaparral Park, the Greenbelt and Camelback corridor)
  • North: 85260 (around the Loop 101 and Frank Lloyd Wright Boulevard), 85255 (DC Ranch, Troon, Pinnacle Peak area), 85266 and 85262 (the far north desert)

Price Comparison by ZIP

Year-to-date single-family medians through October 2026:

South and central

  • 85257: $635,500
  • 85250: $865,000
  • 85251: $917,500

North

  • 85260: $1,080,000
  • 85266: $1,440,000
  • 85262: $1,610,000
  • 85255: $1,732,500

For context, Scottsdale citywide was $1,250,000 year to date. The gap between 85257 and 85255 is more than $1.09 million, which is why I never quote a single "Scottsdale price."

Market Pace by ZIP

October 2026 months of supply and year-to-date days on market:

  • 85257: 4.7 months; 75 days
  • 85250: 4.2 months; 74 days
  • 85251: 5.4 months; 83 days
  • 85260: 3.5 months; 66 days
  • 85255: 3.6 months; 84 days
  • 85266: 5.4 months; 99 days
  • 85262: 7.1 months; 132 days

The takeaway: 85260 and 85255 are the tightest of this group, while 85262 gives buyers the most choice and the most time.

Condos and Townhomes by ZIP

If you're shopping attached homes, the spread is just as wide. Year-to-date townhouse/condo medians through October 2026:

  • 85257: $268,250 on 96 sales
  • 85251: $400,400 on 498 sales, the busiest condo ZIP in this group
  • 85260: $452,500 on 223 sales
  • 85250: $490,750 on 213 sales
  • 85255: $775,000 on 207 sales

85262 and 85266 had only 58 and 20 condo sales year to date, so their medians swing too much to compare fairly.

Questions I Ask Clients Choosing Between Them

  • Where do you drive most often? Work, school, airport and family visits all shift the math
  • Lot or lifestyle? Bigger lots and desert views lean north; walkable dining and the Greenbelt lean south
  • New or renovated? North skews toward newer construction; south and central offer more remodel potential, including mid-century modern homes in 85250
  • What's your all-in budget? Include HOA dues, which vary widely by community

Lifestyle: Preserve vs. Greenbelt

North Scottsdale

  • The McDowell Sonoran Preserve spans more than 30,500 acres with over 230 miles of multi-use trails, per the City of Scottsdale
  • Pinnacle Peak Park offers a 2-mile (one-way) trail on 150 acres
  • Larger lots, more custom and newer construction, and quieter desert streets

South and central Scottsdale

  • The Indian Bend Wash Greenbelt runs 11 miles through the heart of the city, with more than 24 grade-separated crossings
  • Old Town dining, galleries and Scottsdale Stadium within a short drive or ride
  • More mid-century ranch homes, remodel opportunities and townhome options

In 85250, that means mornings at Chaparral Park and evenings minutes from Old Town.

“I live in Chaparral Park, so I'm biased toward the Greenbelt, but I sell all over Scottsdale and I tell clients the same thing: drive both at 7 a.m. and 6 p.m. North Scottsdale wins on space and desert views. South and central win on convenience. Most of my buyers know which one feels right within an hour of touring.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Is north or south Scottsdale better?

Neither across the board. North offers space and Preserve access at higher prices; south and central offer Old Town and the Greenbelt at lower medians.

What is considered south Scottsdale?

There's no official boundary. Here, it means 85257, 85251 and 85250.

Is north Scottsdale cooler than Phoenix?

Often a bit cooler at night. UA Extension notes local temperatures vary with elevation and pavement.

How much more expensive is north Scottsdale?

North ZIP medians ran $1,080,000 to $1,732,500 year to date; south and central ran $635,500 to $917,500.
