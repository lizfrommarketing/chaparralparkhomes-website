The Best Wine Bars Near 85250: Saint Urban and Scottsdale's Growing Wine Scene

Is there actually a good wine scene in Scottsdale? A Chaparral Park resident's tour of the wine bars worth knowing.

One of the questions I get most often from buyers relocating to Scottsdale is some version of: “Is there actually a good wine scene here, or is it all resort bars?” My answer is always the same — yes, and it's grown significantly in the last few years, with some of the best spots sitting right in or around 85250.

Here's a tour of the wine bars worth knowing about if you're considering a move to Chaparral Park or the surrounding area.

Saint Urban: The Neighborhood Favorite, Right in 85250

Saint Urban is the wine bar I send almost every client to first — because it's genuinely in the neighborhood. Located on Hayden Road in the McCormick Ranch area, Saint Urban sits right inside the broader 85250 footprint, making it one of the few true walkable-or-quick-drive wine bar options for Chaparral Park residents.

Named after the patron saint of winemakers, Saint Urban is the creation of chef Christopher Collins, who also runs several other well-regarded Valley restaurants. The wine bar features a rotating selection of roughly 30 premium wines by the glass — leaning West Coast, with selections from California, Oregon, and beyond — alongside a shareable menu built around West Coast-inspired small plates. Think West Coast oysters served three ways, a raw fish “Salt Block” with sushi-grade salmon, tuna, or hamachi, and a Santa Maria-style BBQ tri-tip that's become a signature dish.

What I appreciate most about Saint Urban, and what I tell clients, is the pricing philosophy. Wines by the glass run in the $10 to $14 range, with happy hour pricing dropping even lower — a deliberate choice by the owner to make it a place you can visit several times a week rather than save for special occasions. For a neighborhood that's home to a lot of move-up buyers and young families, that approachability matters.

The covered patio, surrounded by greenery with oversized communal tables, makes it a regular spot for both date nights and larger group gatherings — something I see firsthand as a resident, not just an agent talking up the neighborhood.

FnB: Old Town's Wine Bar Institution

A short drive south in Old Town Scottsdale, FnB has built a reputation as one of the best wine bars in the entire Valley, anchored by James Beard Award-winning chef Charleen Badman's seasonally inspired, vegetable-forward cooking. It's a smaller, more intimate restaurant-meets-wine-bar experience, and it's worth the trip for anyone serious about wine pairing done right.

The Wine Collective of Scottsdale

For residents who want to build a home collection in addition to drinking out, The Wine Collective of Scottsdale offers a boutique tasting room and bottle shop specializing in Arizona wines, with knowledgeable staff trained on every label they carry. Monthly “Meet the Winemaker” events make it a great option for buyers who want to get more involved in Arizona's growing wine industry.

Postino: The Reliable Crowd-Pleaser

Postino's various Valley locations have become something of a local institution, known for an extensive wine list, a living-room-like atmosphere, and bruschetta boards that have practically become a trademark. It's an easy, low-pressure option for groups and remains one of the most consistently recommended wine bars across Scottsdale.

Why the Wine Scene Matters for 85250 Buyers

I bring up the wine bar scene with clients more than you'd expect, because it tells a story about a neighborhood's maturity. Wine bars — unlike sports bars or big chain restaurants — tend to follow rooftops and household income, not the other way around. Saint Urban choosing the McCormick Ranch and 85250 area specifically is a signal about who already lives here and who's moving in.

For buyers who care about having a sophisticated, walkable-adjacent night-out option without driving deep into Old Town every time, having Saint Urban essentially in the neighborhood is a genuine quality-of-life differentiator that few other Scottsdale zip codes can claim.
