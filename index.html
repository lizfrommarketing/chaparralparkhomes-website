Common Questions

Chaparral Park, Answered

Where is Chaparral Park, and what is in it?

Chaparral Park is a roughly 100-acre City of Scottsdale park at 5401 N. Hayden Road in the 85250 zip code. It includes a lake with a fishing pier, Scottsdale’s most popular dog park, a playground, an aquatic center, a community center, sports fields and reservable ramadas, and it connects to the Indian Bend Wash greenbelt path.

What are the dog park hours?

The Chaparral dog park is open from 5:30 a.m. to 10 p.m., and the park itself is open from sunrise to 10:30 p.m.

Which school district serves Chaparral Park?

Chaparral Park and the 85250 zip code are served by the Scottsdale Unified School District (SUSD). Specific school assignments depend on the street, so confirm with the district before you buy — Elizabeth can help you check any address.

How close is Chaparral Park to Old Town Scottsdale?

About ten minutes, and close enough to walk or bike from some blocks. Scottsdale Fashion Square and Old Town dining are a short trip away.
