McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park Holiday Lights 2026: Dates, Tickets & a Local's Guide for 85250

Holiday Lights at McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park runs Nov. 27-Dec. 30, 2026; tickets ($15) go on sale Oct. 27 at 8 a.m. Plus Spook-Track-Ula and park basics in 85250.

When is Holiday Lights at McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park? The park lists Holiday Lights 2026 for November 27 through December 30, nightly from 5:30 to 9:30 p.m., with no event December 24 or 25. Tickets are $15 for ages 3 and up (2 and under free), and they go on sale Tuesday, October 27, at 8 a.m. through the City's ActiveNet system.

Why does that sale date matter? Because this is one of the most-searched family events in Scottsdale, and the park itself urges guests to set up an ActiveNet account ahead of time. If you live in 85250, the park is practically in your neighborhood, on Indian Bend Road. Here's what to know, plus the last week of Spook-Track-Ula.

Data note: Event dates, hours and prices come from the McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park's official events, Holiday Lights, Spook-Track-Ula and rides pages (checked Oct. 22, 2026). The Holiday Lights page's headline reads 2026, but one detail line still shows 2025, so confirm dates before you buy. Weekday of Oct. 27 verified by calendar.

Holiday Lights 2026 at a Glance

  • Dates: Nov. 27 - Dec. 30 (no event Dec. 24-25)
  • Hours: 5:30 - 9:30 p.m.
  • Price: $15 per person, ages 3+; free for 2 and under
  • Included: train and carousel rides; wristbands required for all riders
  • Tickets on sale: Oct. 27, 8 a.m., via ActiveNet
  • Extras: S'more Land fire pits with $8 s'more kits, Hartley's General Store and food trucks, and the Model Railroad Building
  • Santa & Friends / the Grinch: dates and times TBD per the park

Tips From the Neighborhood

  • Create your ActiveNet account now. The park specifically recommends it to speed checkout
  • Pick weeknights if your schedule allows; the park lists the same hours every night of the run
  • Dress in layers. Desert evenings in December cool off quickly after sunset
  • Budget for extras. S'mores, food and drinks are sold separately

Still Time for Spook-Track-Ula (Through Oct. 31)

  • Dates and hours: Oct. 1 - 31, 6 - 9:30 p.m.
  • Timed entry: arrival windows at 6, 6:30, 7, 7:30 and 8 p.m.
  • Price: $15 per person; free for 2 and under
  • Included: the Paradise & Pacific spooky train, carousel rides, a haunted train walk-through (new this year, best for older kids and adults) and a storybook train ride for little ones
  • Tickets: online only through ActiveNet

McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park: The Basics

  • Address: 7301 E. Indian Bend Rd., Scottsdale, AZ 85250
  • Phone: 480-312-2312
  • Size: a 30-acre park run by the City of Scottsdale's Parks & Recreation Department

Rides and Attractions

  • Paradise & Pacific Railroad: a 5/12-scale reproduction of a Colorado narrow-gauge railroad and the park's original attraction
  • Scottsdale Charros Carousel: a restored 1950 Allan Herschell carousel
  • Arboretum Train: weekend loops through a two-acre desert arboretum
  • Model Railroad Building: 10,000 square feet of miniature trains
  • Scottsdale Railroad Museum, playgrounds, a splash pad and the Western Town play structure

Regular Ride Prices

  • $3 per ride ticket (one per rider; 2 and under ride free)
  • 8 tickets for $20
  • All-day wristband: $12
  • Train and carousel run every half hour on weekdays and continuously on weekends

Coming Up After the Holidays

  • Noon Year's Eve: Dec. 31, 11 a.m. - 1 p.m., a midday countdown for families
