How to Reset a Garbage Disposal: Humming, Jammed or Dead, Plus What Not to Put Down It This Thanksgiving

Disposal humming or dead before Thanksgiving? InSinkErator's reset steps (Allen wrench, red button), and Scottsdale Water's rules for grease, gravy and food scraps.

How do you reset a garbage disposal? Turn the switch off, then press the small red overload button on the bottom of the unit. If the motor hums but won't spin, free the jam first with a 1/4-inch Allen wrench in the center hole underneath. Those are InSinkErator's own instructions, and they fix most dead or humming disposals in minutes.

Why now? Thanksgiving is one week away, and holiday cooking is when grease, peels and gravy go down the drain. Scottsdale Water's advice is blunt: garbage disposals make grease clogs worse.

Data note: Reset and jam steps are from InSinkErator's "Fixing a Jammed Garbage Disposal" support page. Grease guidance is from Scottsdale Water's maintenance page and its "FOG Clogs" brochure. Read November 19, 2026.

First, Listen: What Is Your Disposal Telling You?

  • It hums but doesn't spin: the motor has power but the plate is stuck. That's a jam
  • It's completely silent: the overload protector may have tripped, or no power is reaching it
  • It stopped mid-grind: usually a jam that then tripped the overload

Safety first: InSinkErator says to make certain the switch is off, and never put your hand in the disposal. If the sink is full of water, let it drain or bail it out.

How to Fix a Humming or Jammed Disposal

InSinkErator ships every unit with a silver crank-shaped wrench about four inches long. If you can't find it, a standard 1/4-inch Allen wrench works.

  1. Insert the wrench into the hole at the dead center of the bottom of the disposal, facing the floor
  2. Work it back and forth in both directions. Some force is fine; InSinkErator says the wrench is designed to bend before the disposal is damaged
  3. Keep going until it turns freely in complete circles both ways
  4. Check the red overload button (next section)
  5. Look inside with a flashlight. Remove anything you see with tongs or pliers, never fingers
  6. Turn on the water, then the disposal. If it jams again, repeat

How to Reset the Red Button

The overload protector is a small square red button on the bottom of the unit.

  • If it has tripped, it sits about a quarter inch (5 mm) lower than normal
  • Press it gently
  • If it won't stay up, wait ten minutes and try again

No reset button?

Some InSinkErator models (EZ Connect, Builder Series, Amana and ES units) have an automatic overload switch instead. Turn the wall switch off, or unplug a push-button air-switch model, and let it cool for up to 20 minutes. If it still won't run, check your breaker panel for a tripped breaker or blown fuse.

What Not to Put Down the Disposal This Thanksgiving

Scottsdale Water's "FOG Clogs" brochure lists the fats, oils and grease that cause trouble, and many are holiday staples:

  • Gravy and sauces
  • Butter, margarine, lard and shortening
  • Dairy, milk and cream
  • Mayonnaise and salad dressing
  • Baked goods and frosting

Scottsdale Water's three habits

  • Pour cooking oil and grease into a small container with absorbent material, such as paper towels or coffee grounds, and put it in the trash
  • Scrape plates into the trash, not the disposal, and wipe greasy pans with a paper towel before washing
  • Cover the sink drain with a strainer and limit disposal use

Hot water doesn't fix it. The city notes that hot grease cools and solidifies further down your pipes. InSinkErator agrees: run the disposal with a moderate flow of cold water, keep it running a few seconds after grinding, and never pour grease or fat into the disposal.

Why it matters: Scottsdale Water says that, depending on where a clog is, the resident or property owner may be responsible for cleaning or repairing the line, plus damage from a backup. The brochure also cites an EPA estimate that 65% of sewer spills are caused by fats, oils and grease.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my garbage disposal?

Switch off, then press the red overload button on the bottom. If it won't stay up, wait ten minutes and try again.

Why is my garbage disposal humming but not working?

It's jammed. Use a 1/4-inch Allen wrench in the bottom center hole until it turns freely both ways.

How do I reset a garbage disposal with no reset button?

Turn it off or unplug it, wait up to 20 minutes, then check the breaker.

What should you not put down a garbage disposal?

Grease, fat, oil, and grease-heavy foods like gravy, butter and dressings.
