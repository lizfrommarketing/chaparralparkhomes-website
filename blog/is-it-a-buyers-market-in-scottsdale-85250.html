Is It a Buyer's Market in Scottsdale? What October 2026 Inventory Says for the City, ZIP 85250 and Condos

Is it a buyer's market in Scottsdale? Single-family homes sat at 4.2 months of supply in October 2026, a balanced market, while condos hit 6.6 months. What it means in 85250.

Is it a buyer's market in Scottsdale? For single-family homes, not quite: Scottsdale is in balanced territory, with 4.2 months of supply in October 2026. For townhouses and condos, the answer leans yes. Scottsdale condos had 6.6 months of supply, above the six-month line that generally favors buyers.

In ZIP 85250, single-family homes also sat at 4.2 months of supply, while condo and townhouse supply jumped to 5.7 months from 3.8 a year earlier. Translation: buyers have more room to negotiate than they did a year ago, and condo buyers have the most.

Data note: Market figures are from the Phoenix REALTORS® Local Market Updates for Scottsdale, ZIP 85250 and Phoenix, October 2026 (ARMLS data, current as of Nov. 3, 2026), the latest published. The balanced-market range is from the Congressional Research Service. Rates are from Freddie Mac's PMMS, Nov. 25, 2026. Read November 27, 2026.

How to Tell a Buyer's Market From a Seller's Market

The cleanest single signal is months of supply: how long today's listings would last at the current pace of sales if no new homes came on the market.

  • Under 4 months: a shortage, which generally favors sellers
  • 4 to 6 months: the range many housing economists call balanced
  • Over 6 months: a surplus, which generally favors buyers

The Congressional Research Service adds an important caveat: what counts as balanced can differ from place to place. So read months of supply alongside days on market, the share of list price sellers receive and pending sales.

Scottsdale in October 2026: The Numbers

Single-family homes

  • Months of supply: 4.2, down from 4.4 a year earlier
  • Inventory: 1,480 homes, essentially flat
  • Median sales price: $1,225,000, up 5.6%
  • Days on market until sale: 75, down from 82
  • Percent of list price received: 96.8%
  • Pending sales: 208, down 43.5% from 368

Townhouses and condos

  • Months of supply: 6.6, up from 5.5
  • Inventory: 1,127 units, up 12.0%
  • Median sales price: $485,000, up 4.3%
  • Days on market until sale: 78
  • Percent of list price received: 96.6%

The read: single-family prices are still rising, but buyers are writing fewer contracts. Condos have crossed into buyer-leaning territory.

ZIP 85250 in October 2026

85250 is a small sample, so one month can swing. The Phoenix REALTORS report itself warns that percent changes can look extreme with few sales.

Single-family homes

  • Months of supply: 4.2, unchanged from a year earlier
  • Inventory: 51 homes, down from 55
  • Median sales price: $825,000, down 7.2% on 13 closings
  • Year-to-date median: $865,000, up 6.8%
  • Days on market until sale: 89
  • Percent of list price received: 96.8%
  • Pending sales: 4, down from 16

Townhouses and condos

  • Months of supply: 5.7, up from 3.8
  • Inventory: 113 units, up 22.8%
  • Median sales price: $475,000

What This Means If You're Buying

  • Negotiate on terms, not just price. Sellers received about 97 cents on the dollar in October, so the bigger wins are often credits, repairs or a rate buydown. See seller concessions and rate buydowns
  • Watch days on market. A single-family listing well past the 89-day 85250 median is a good candidate for a below-list offer. Our guide to how much below asking to offer walks through it
  • Budget for rates. Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 7.21% on Nov. 25, 2026, down from 7.28% the week before
  • Condos give you the most leverage, with supply above six months citywide

What This Means If You're Selling

  • Price to the comps, not to the headline median. Balanced markets punish overpricing with time
  • Expect a negotiation. Plan for an offer below list and requests after inspection
  • Presentation matters more when buyers have choices. Start with how to price your house

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Scottsdale a buyer's or seller's market right now?

Balanced for single-family homes at 4.2 months of supply; buyer-leaning for condos at 6.6 months.

Is Phoenix a buyer's or seller's market?

Phoenix single-family homes were also at 4.2 months, up from 3.6. Phoenix condos were at 6.7 months.

How many months of inventory is a buyer's market?

More than six months is generally considered a buyer's market; four to six is balanced.

Can I negotiate on a house in 85250 right now?

Often. Sellers received 96.8% of list on average, and pending sales were down sharply.
