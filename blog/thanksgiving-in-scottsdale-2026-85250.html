Thanksgiving in Scottsdale 2026: Dinner Reservations, Morning Hikes, Trash Pickup Changes and the Weekend Ahead in 85250

Thanksgiving in Scottsdale is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026: where to book dinner, the Turkey Trot and a free hike, trash pickup shifts, city closures and Scottsdazzle on Nov. 28.

What should you know about Thanksgiving in Scottsdale this year? Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026. City offices close Thursday and Friday, there's no trash collection on Thanksgiving Day (Thursday routes run Friday and Friday routes run Saturday), and the best-known restaurant tables are already filling. Axios Scottsdale reports the Fairmont Scottsdale Princess is using a waitlist.

The weekend at a glance: a Thanksgiving-morning 5K on Scottsdale Road in 85250, a free guided hike, Old Town ArtWalk that evening, the start of the Mesa Temple Christmas lights on Friday and the Scottsdazzle Tree Lighting on Saturday, Nov. 28. Here's the plan, with sources.

Data note: Details come from the City of Scottsdale's holiday collection schedule, Axios Scottsdale (Nov. 10, 2026), the Scottsdale Turkey Trot, the McDowell Sonoran Conservancy, the Old Town Scottsdale events calendar, Experience Scottsdale and MesaTemple.org. Read November 13, 2026. Event details can change; confirm before you go.

Thanksgiving Week Logistics

Trash and recycling (City of Scottsdale):

  • Thursday, Nov. 26: no collection
  • Thursday routes move to Friday, Nov. 27
  • Friday routes move to Saturday, Nov. 28
  • Monday through Wednesday routes run as usual
  • The city asks residents to have containers out by 5 a.m. on collection day or after 6 p.m. the evening before

City offices: closed Thursday, Nov. 26 and Friday, Nov. 27.

Need your regular day? See our Scottsdale trash pickup schedule guide.

Where to Book Thanksgiving Dinner

From Axios Scottsdale's Nov. 10 roundup:

  • The Scottsdale Resort & Spa: turkey, prime rib, seafood and pie in the Coronado Ballroom, $105, 10 a.m.-3 p.m.
  • McCormick Ranch Golf Club: holiday buffet with golf-course and Camelback views, $60, 11 a.m.-5 p.m.
  • The Americano and Weft & Warp: plated dinners at $95
  • La Fogata: three courses, $79, 4-9 p.m.
  • Chompie's Scottsdale: a $33 turkey plate, dine in or take home, 10 a.m.-4 p.m.

For something different (also from Axios):

  • Kauboi: a $145 Japanese-inspired buffet with sushi, yakitori, turkey and brisket
  • Sexy Roman: a $125 Italian-inspired buffet with tableside pasta tossed in a cheese wheel
  • Cala Scottsdale: maple-brined turkey and focaccia-squash stuffing, from $65
  • Mastro's Steakhouse: steaks and seafood, 1-9 p.m.
  • Fleming's at DC Ranch: turkey, filet or ribeye, from $64

Tip: reserve this week. If you're hosting at home, order pies and sides early too.

Thanksgiving Morning: Move Before the Meal

Scottsdale Turkey Trot (in 85250)

  • 8:30 a.m., Thursday, Nov. 26
  • Embassy Suites Scottsdale, 5001 N. Scottsdale Road
  • Chip-timed 5K, walkers and strollers welcome, wellness festival after
  • On-site parking is limited and first come, first served

More in our Scottsdale Turkey Trot 2026 guide.

More Pie Please Thanksgiving Hike

  • 9-10:30 a.m., free, from Lost Dog Wash Trailhead
  • 2.5 miles, rated moderate, led by the McDowell Sonoran Conservancy
  • RSVP required, space is limited, no dogs

Closer to home, the Indian Bend Wash greenbelt makes an easy walk from Chaparral Park.

Thursday Evening and the Holiday Weekend

  • Thursday, Nov. 26, 7-9 p.m.: ArtWalk in Old Town's Arts District, per the Old Town events calendar
  • Friday, Nov. 27: the Mesa Temple Christmas Lights begin, 5-10 p.m. nightly through Dec. 30, free
  • Saturday, Nov. 28: Scottsdazzle opens with the Tree Lighting Spectacular, celebrating 75 years of Scottsdale, with live music, food vendors and a 36-foot tree; Experience Scottsdale also lists Small Business Saturday giveaways

Scottsdazzle runs through Dec. 31. See our Scottsdazzle 2026 guide and Scottsdale Christmas markets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there trash pickup on Thanksgiving in Scottsdale?

No. Thursday routes move to Friday and Friday routes move to Saturday.

Where can I eat Thanksgiving dinner in Scottsdale?

Axios lists The Scottsdale Resort & Spa, McCormick Ranch Golf Club, Chompie's and more. Book early.

What is there to do in Scottsdale on Thanksgiving morning?

The Turkey Trot 5K (8:30 a.m., Embassy Suites) or the free More Pie Please hike (9 a.m., RSVP).

Are Scottsdale city offices open the day after Thanksgiving?

No. They're closed Thursday, Nov. 26 and Friday, Nov. 27.
