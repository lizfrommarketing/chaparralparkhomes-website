Scottsdale Christmas Markets 2026: Dates, Hours and Locations for Holiday Shopping Near 85250

Six Scottsdale holiday markets from Nov. 21 to Dec. 13, 2026, including the Merry Merchantile Market and a Christmas market inside 85250. Dates, hours and tips.

Where are the Christmas markets in Scottsdale in 2026? The biggest is the free Merry Merchantile Market, Dec. 12-13 at Scottsdale Civic Center in Old Town. Others include the Scottsdale Sparkle Christmas Market (Nov. 27), the St. Maria Goretti Christmas Market inside 85250 (Dec. 4-5) and The Market: Holiday Edition at WestWorld (Dec. 12-13).

For Chaparral Park neighbors, the shortest trips are Old Town's Civic Center and St. Maria Goretti on Granite Reef Road. Below are six markets in date order, with hours, addresses and what organizers say to expect.

Data note: Dates, hours and details are from each organizer's own listing: The Merchantile Market, Arizona Boardwalk, St. Maria Goretti Catholic Church (Eventbrite) and the WestWorld of Scottsdale events calendar. Read November 11, 2026. Plans can change, so confirm before you go.

2026 Scottsdale Holiday Markets at a Glance

  • Sat., Nov. 21: Grateful Gathering Merchantile Market, Scottsdale Civic Center
  • Fri., Nov. 27: Scottsdale Sparkle Christmas Market, Arizona Boardwalk
  • Fri.-Sat., Dec. 4-5: St. Maria Goretti Christmas Market, 85250
  • Sat., Dec. 12: Holiday Fair & Marketplace, Arizona Boardwalk
  • Sat.-Sun., Dec. 12-13: Merry Merchantile Market, Scottsdale Civic Center
  • Sat.-Sun., Dec. 12-13: The Market: Holiday Edition, WestWorld of Scottsdale

Old Town: Scottsdale Civic Center Markets

Grateful Gathering Merchantile Market (Nov. 21)

  • When: Saturday, Nov. 21, 10 a.m.-5 p.m.
  • Where: Scottsdale Civic Center, 3939 N. Drinkwater Blvd.
  • Cost: free admission and free parking
  • What to expect: 150+ local vendors, free live concerts, food vendors, craft beer and cocktails and a free kids zone
  • Extra: an optional $15 tote, sold online, bundles vendor discounts and in-store perks

Merry Merchantile Market (Dec. 12-13)

  • When: Saturday, Dec. 12, 10 a.m.-5 p.m.; Sunday, Dec. 13, 10 a.m.-4 p.m.
  • Where: Scottsdale Civic Center
  • Cost: free admission and parking; pet friendly
  • Santa photos: 10 a.m.-2 p.m. both days
  • Carolers: the Jingle Belles stroll 10 a.m.-noon; the Grinch appears noon-2 p.m.
  • Live music: TEMPO and Joey Gutos on Saturday; Sedona Red and Chris Lawrie on Sunday
  • Also: free kids' holiday crafts, face painting and specialty food and drinks

Organizers say both markets go on rain or shine and are stroller and wheelchair accessible.

Inside 85250: St. Maria Goretti Christmas Market (Dec. 4-5)

  • Where: St. Maria Goretti Catholic Church, 6261 N. Granite Reef Road, Scottsdale 85250
  • When: the Eventbrite listing runs from 9 a.m. Friday, Dec. 4 to 4 p.m. Saturday, Dec. 5; check the listing for daily hours
  • What to expect: a two-day holiday market with vendors, food and family activities, per the organizer

This is the closest market to most Chaparral Park homes and an easy one to pair with errands on the south side of the ZIP.

Arizona Boardwalk Markets (Nov. 27 and Dec. 12)

Both are at the Arizona Boardwalk Courtyard, 9500 E. Vía de Ventura, and listed as free.

Scottsdale Sparkle Christmas Market

  • When: Friday, Nov. 27, 10 a.m.-4 p.m.
  • What to expect: 60+ vendors, plus character appearances, face painting, glitter tattoos and slime art for kids

Holiday Fair & Marketplace

  • When: Saturday, Dec. 12, 10 a.m.-2 p.m.
  • What to expect: professional Santa photos, a "snow blizzard," live entertainment, bounce houses and slides, face painting and a $500 holiday shopping-spree giveaway

North Scottsdale: The Market: Holiday Edition (Dec. 12-13)

  • Where: WestWorld of Scottsdale, 16601 N. Pima Road
  • When: Saturday-Sunday, Dec. 12-13; the WestWorld calendar lists an 8 a.m. start
  • What to expect: a new holiday event from Junk in the Trunk Vintage Market with vintage, handmade gifts, home decor and specialty foods
  • Before you go: check the organizer for tickets and parking

Make a Day of It

  • Dec. 12 is a big day. Three markets overlap, so pick one or plan a route
  • Pair Old Town with Scottsdazzle. The city's holiday program runs all season; see our Scottsdazzle 2026 guide
  • Saturday mornings: start at the Old Town Scottsdale Farmers Market, which shares the Civic Center area
  • After dark: the McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park holiday lights are a short drive from Chaparral Park
  • Bring a tote and a card. Vendors set their own payment options

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a Christmas market in Scottsdale in 2026?

Yes: six on our list, from Nov. 21 to Dec. 13, led by the Merry Merchantile Market, Dec. 12-13.

When is the Old Town Scottsdale Christmas market?

Dec. 12 (10 a.m.-5 p.m.) and Dec. 13 (10 a.m.-4 p.m.) at Scottsdale Civic Center.

Is the Scottsdale Sparkle Christmas Market free?

Yes. It's Nov. 27, 10 a.m.-4 p.m., at Arizona Boardwalk.

Is there a Christmas market in North Scottsdale?

The Market: Holiday Edition, Dec. 12-13 at WestWorld of Scottsdale.
