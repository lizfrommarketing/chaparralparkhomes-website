Scottsdale Housing Market Forecast 2027: What the Latest Data Says for 85250 Owners and Buyers

Will Scottsdale home prices drop in 2027? Fannie Mae's Nov. 2026 forecast, 7.11% rates and October 2026 Scottsdale and 85250 data point to a flat, patient market.

What's the forecast for the Scottsdale housing market in 2027? The best available data points to a flat, patient market, not a crash. Fannie Mae's November 2026 Housing Forecast projects national home prices rising just 0.6% in 2027 with the 30-year rate averaging about 7.2%, while Scottsdale's October 2026 single-family median held at $1,225,000, up 5.6% from a year ago.

What that means locally: fewer buyers are writing contracts, homes are taking longer to sell, and pricing matters more than it has in years. Owners who price to the market should still sell; buyers have more room to negotiate than at any point since rates jumped.

Data note: Forecasts are from Fannie Mae's Economic and Strategic Research Group (Housing Forecast dated Nov. 12, 2026) and NAR's Nov. 6, 2026 outlook. Rates are from Freddie Mac's PMMS (Nov. 12, 2026). Local figures are from Phoenix REALTORS Local Market Updates for Scottsdale and ZIP 85250, October 2026. Read November 18, 2026.

What the National Forecasts Say

From Fannie Mae's November 2026 Housing Forecast:

  • Home prices: up 2.7% in 2026, slowing to 0.6% in 2027 and 1.1% in 2028 (fourth quarter over fourth quarter)
  • Mortgage rates: the 30-year fixed averaging 7.2% in every quarter of 2027
  • Existing-home sales: about 3.99 million in 2027, down 0.4% from 2026, before a projected rebound to 4.19 million in 2028

NAR Chief Economist Lawrence Yun, speaking Nov. 6, said months' supply nationally is at its highest level since 2015, and that "with longer days on market, you have to be realistic to the market condition."

Rates today are already near that forecast: Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 7.11% on Nov. 12, 2026, versus 6.24% a year earlier.

Where Scottsdale Stands Going Into 2027

Phoenix REALTORS' October 2026 numbers for Scottsdale single-family homes:

  • Median sales price: $1,225,000, up 5.6%; year to date $1,250,000, up 5.9%
  • Closed sales: 325, down 12.9%
  • Pending sales: 208, down 43.5%
  • Inventory: 1,480 homes, up 0.1%, or 4.2 months of supply
  • Days on market: 75, and sellers received 96.8% of list price

The split matters. Prices are still higher than a year ago, but the contract pipeline thinned sharply in October, which tends to show up in closings over the next few months.

The 85250 Picture

ZIP 85250 is a smaller sample, so single months swing more. October 2026 for single-family homes:

  • Median sales price: $825,000 on 13 closings, down 7.2% from October 2025; year to date $865,000, up 6.8%
  • Inventory: 51 homes, 4.2 months of supply
  • Pending sales: 4, versus 16 a year earlier
  • Days on market: 89; 96.8% of list price received

Townhouses and condos in 85250 had a $475,000 median, but supply rose to 5.7 months from 3.8, so condo buyers have more choice heading into winter.

My Read for 2027

If you're selling

Price for the market you're in, not the one from spring. Year-to-date medians are up, but buyers are paying about 3% under list on average, and homes that miss on price sit. Our how to price your house guide walks through the comps I'd use.

If you're buying

A flat forecast means you don't need to rush, but it also doesn't promise a discount for waiting. If the payment works at today's rate, negotiating on price, repairs or a rate buydown is realistic right now.

If you're staying put

Most 85250 owners have built equity over the last several years, and the forecasts don't point to a reversal. Use the slower season for projects that protect value.

“When I look at 85250 heading into 2027, I see a market that rewards preparation. The homes that are clean, well-priced and photographed in good light are still selling to my buyers. The ones that are priced on hope are the ones I see sitting into a second and third month. I'd rather help a client get the price right on day one than chase the market down.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Will home prices drop in 2027?

National forecasts don't call for a decline. Fannie Mae projects 0.6% growth in 2027, close to flat.

Is the Scottsdale housing market going to crash?

October data shows prices up year over year and supply steady at 4.2 months. It's slower and more negotiable, not collapsing.

What will mortgage rates be in 2027?

Fannie Mae projects about 7.2% for the 30-year fixed through 2027. The Nov. 12 actual was 7.11%.

Is 2027 a good time to buy a house in Scottsdale?

It can be if the payment fits now. Flat prices and below-list sales give buyers room to negotiate.
