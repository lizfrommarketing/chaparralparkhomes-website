Does Scottsdale Flood? What the Oct. 30-Nov. 1 Storm Showed About the Indian Bend Wash and 85250

Scottsdale got 4.25 inches of rain Oct. 30-Nov. 1, and water filled the Indian Bend Wash near Hayden and Chaparral. How the greenbelt works and what 85250 owners should know.

Does Scottsdale flood? Yes, in places, during big storms, and the Indian Bend Wash is designed to take that water. KTAR reported 4.25 inches of rain in Scottsdale from 8 a.m. Friday, Oct. 30, through Sunday, Nov. 1, and on Monday morning Arizona's Family showed floodwater covering roads and walking paths near Hayden and Chaparral roads, right at the edge of Chaparral Park.

What it means for 85250: the greenbelt that floods is the same chain of parks, golf courses and paths that makes this ZIP code so livable. The Flood Control District of Maricopa County describes the Indian Bend Wash as a flood control channel that provides 100-year flood protection for a 65-square-mile drainage area. Here's how it works, and what owners and buyers should do with that knowledge.

Data note: Rain totals are from KTAR (Nov. 1, 2026, citing ABC15). Storm impacts are from Arizona's Family (Nov. 2, 2026). System details are from the Scottsdale Flood Response Plan prepared by the Flood Control District of Maricopa County and City of Scottsdale stormwater pages. Read November 16, 2026.

The Storm by the Numbers

  • Scottsdale: 4.25 inches, 8 a.m. Friday to Sunday (KTAR, citing ABC15)
  • Phoenix Sky Harbor: 0.92 inches over the same window
  • Halloween: Phoenix saw its first Halloween rainfall since 1987, per KTAR
  • Near Hayden and Chaparral: floodwater over roads and paths Monday morning, per Arizona's Family
  • A rescue: a driver was pulled from a flooded wash near Scottsdale and Westland roads Sunday evening

For context, the city's stormwater FAQ says a 100-year storm requires every part of a watershed to receive 3.4 inches within six hours. Our storm was spread over roughly three days.

How the Indian Bend Wash Works

Per the Flood Response Plan, the project runs from Indian Bend Road south to the Salt River and has five parts: a greenbelt, an inlet channel, an outlet channel, an interceptor channel, and collector and side channels.

  • The greenbelt: a 4.5-mile grassy swale between McDonald Drive and McKellips Road with playgrounds, golf courses, trails and ball fields
  • Built by: a partnership of the Flood Control District, the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, the City of Scottsdale and private developers
  • Completed: 1985
  • Drainage area: 65 square miles, including parts of Scottsdale and Tempe

In other words, when the paths near Chaparral Park go underwater, the channel is carrying water away from homes. It's why the Greenbelt is both a flood channel and the reason so many people love living here.

Getting Around When the Wash Runs

The city's alternate routes page says most south Scottsdale problem spots are tied to the wash as it affects Hayden Road and the major east-west streets that cross it.

  • Alternate routes: the city suggests Pima Road and Scottsdale Road
  • Turn around, don't drown: Scottsdale Fire Capt. Dave Folio told Arizona's Family that "just a few inches of moving water can cause you to lose control of your vehicle"
  • Barricades mean closed: never drive around them or assume a wash is shallow
  • Check conditions: the city's wash flow and rainfall page links to real-time gauge data from the Flood Control District

What 85250 Owners and Buyers Should Do

  • Know your zone: the city posts FEMA flood maps; read our guide to buying in a flood zone in Scottsdale
  • Find your elevation certificate: if your house was built after 1990, the city says it should have one on file
  • Maintain your drainage: the city says private drainage facilities are generally the homeowner's or HOA's responsibility, even in a city easement
  • After the storm: walk your lot, clear scuppers and side-yard channels, and photograph any ponding
  • Report problems: 480-312-5000 for emergency street flooding; 480-312-2500 for Stormwater Management

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Scottsdale flood?

Some streets can flood or close in severe storms. In south Scottsdale, most problem spots are tied to the Indian Bend Wash near Hayden Road.

Why does the Indian Bend Wash greenbelt flood?

It's a flood control channel by design, protecting a 65-square-mile drainage area.

How much rain fell in Scottsdale during the Oct. 30-Nov. 1, 2026 storm?

4.25 inches, per KTAR citing ABC15. Sky Harbor recorded 0.92 inches.

Who do I call about flooding in Scottsdale?

Police at 480-312-5000 for emergency street flooding; Stormwater Management at 480-312-2500.
