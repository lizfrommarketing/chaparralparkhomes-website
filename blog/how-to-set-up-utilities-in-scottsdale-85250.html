How to Set Up Utilities in Scottsdale: Water, Trash, Electric and Gas for Your 85250 Move

Moving to 85250? Start City of Scottsdale water, sewer and trash with one request, check whether SRP or APS serves your address, and set up gas and internet before closing.

How do you set up utilities in Scottsdale? Start with the city, then the power company, then everything else. The City of Scottsdale handles water, sewer and residential trash and recycling, and you request service online after confirming the city serves your address. Electricity comes from SRP or APS depending on the address, natural gas (if the home has it) from Southwest Gas, and internet from the provider you choose.

When to do it: about one to two weeks before closing, with a start date of your closing or possession day. Homeowners give the city escrow information instead of a deposit, so have your title company's details handy.

Data note: Requirements come from the City of Scottsdale's Utility Service Start Service, Utilities and Solid Waste pages and SRP's service-area page. Read November 13, 2026.

Your Scottsdale Utility Checklist

  • Water, sewer, trash and recycling: City of Scottsdale (one city account)
  • Electricity: SRP or APS, by address
  • Natural gas: Southwest Gas, if the home has a gas line
  • Internet, TV and phone: your choice of provider; check availability by address
  • HOA: if the home has one, the HOA or its management company for dues and gate or amenity access

Step 1: City of Scottsdale Water, Sewer and Trash

Open the city's Utility Service - Start Service page.

  • Verify the address first; the form confirms the City of Scottsdale serves the property
  • Homeowners: the city requires escrow information and doesn't require a deposit
  • Renters: no deposit if you've had City of Scottsdale service for at least 12 months within the last two years with no more than two delinquencies in the most recent 12 months; otherwise a deposit is billed
  • Deposit questions: utility billing at 480-312-2461 (staff are unavailable noon to 1 p.m. on weekdays)

After you're set up:

  • Create an online utilities account for paperless billing and usage history
  • ACH payments have no fee; card payments carry a $3.50 convenience fee per transaction, per the city
  • Put containers out by 5 a.m. on collection day or after 6 p.m. the night before
  • The city offers moving box collection, plus monthly brush and bulk service

Want to know what your bill will look like? See Scottsdale water rates 2026 and the Scottsdale trash pickup schedule.

Step 2: SRP or APS?

Scottsdale is served by two electric utilities, and the boundary follows addresses, not ZIP codes.

  • Check SRP first: SRP's service-area page lets you enter your address to see which SRP services are available. SRP says it serves more than 2 million customers in and around the Valley
  • If SRP doesn't serve it, the provider is APS
  • Shortcut: ask the seller for a recent bill, or check the disclosure documents in your transaction
  • Rate plans matter: both utilities offer time-of-use plans, so choose one that fits how you run the AC and pool pump

Step 3: Gas, Internet and the Small Stuff

  • Gas: confirm the home has a gas line (water heater, range, fireplace or pool heater) before calling Southwest Gas
  • Internet: schedule installation early; holiday weeks book up
  • Mail: file a change of address with USPS
  • Driver license and vehicle: if you're new to Arizona, check MVD requirements for new residents

Timing Tips for a Smooth Closing

  • Start city service and electricity effective the day you take possession
  • Ask the seller to stop their service the same day, so there's no gap
  • Read the meters (or photograph them) on move-in day
  • For Thanksgiving week, note that City of Scottsdale offices are closed Nov. 26-27, 2026

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start water service in Scottsdale?

Use the city's online Start Service request after verifying your address. Call 480-312-2461 with deposit questions.

Do I have to pay a deposit for Scottsdale water service?

Homeowners provide escrow information instead. Renters may qualify for a waiver based on recent city service history.

Is my Scottsdale home on SRP or APS?

It depends on the address. Use SRP's address lookup; if SRP doesn't serve it, it's APS.

Is trash included in Scottsdale utilities?

Yes. Residential trash and recycling are city services on the same account as water and sewer.
