The Real Reason Families Move to 85250: Scottsdale Unified School District

Families relocating to the Phoenix metro keep asking about school districts. Here's why my answer is 85250 and Scottsdale Unified.

Every week I talk to families relocating to the Phoenix metro area who say the same thing: “We need to be in a good school district.” My answer is always the same: look at 85250.

The 85250 zip code sits inside the Scottsdale Unified School District — and that matters more than most buyers realize until they start doing their research. SUSD isn't just a good school district. It's one of the most consistently high-performing public school districts in Arizona, and homes zoned within it carry a real premium because of it.

Here's everything you need to know about schools and home values in 85250.

What Is Scottsdale Unified School District?

Scottsdale Unified School District (SUSD) serves over 22,000 students across Scottsdale and parts of Phoenix and Paradise Valley. It's one of the largest unified districts in Arizona and one of the most sought-after by families relocating from out of state.

What separates SUSD from other Valley districts isn't just test scores — it's the breadth of programs, the funding, and the community investment that comes from being in one of Arizona's most affluent and education-focused cities.

Most 85250 homes sell between roughly $420K and $775K (median about $600K–$650K, as of October 2026), and at those prices being in SUSD versus a neighboring district can be the difference between a bidding war and a negotiation. Homes in 85250 hold their value in part because the school district doesn't change.

Schools Serving the 85250 Zip Code

Families buying in Chaparral Park and the surrounding 85250 area are typically zoned for Scottsdale Unified schools at every level — elementary, middle, and high school. While specific school assignments depend on your exact address, the quality of education available throughout the zip code is consistently strong.

What SUSD schools are known for:

  • Strong academic programming and extracurriculars
  • Well-maintained facilities
  • Active parent involvement and PTAs
  • Award-winning arts, music, and athletics programs
  • Advanced Placement and honors course offerings at the high school level

For families moving from states like California, Illinois, or the Northeast, SUSD often compares favorably with the districts they're leaving — at a fraction of the property tax burden.

The 85250 School Advantage for Buyers

Here's something I tell every family I work with: the school district you buy into on day one is the school district your kids are in for years. Unlike a renovation or a backyard, you can't change it after closing.

That's why buying in 85250 and the Scottsdale Unified boundary is a long-term decision, not just a quality-of-life one. It's a financial decision too.

Homes in strong school districts statistically sell faster, appreciate more steadily, and attract a larger pool of buyers when it's time to sell. If you buy in 85250 today and sell in five years, you'll be marketing your home to every family in the Phoenix metro who has SUSD on their school district checklist. That list is long.

Families Love 85250 for More Than the Schools

I want to be honest: the schools are often what gets families in the door, but the lifestyle is what makes them stay.

The Chaparral Park neighborhood puts families within walking distance of one of Scottsdale's best parks, the Indian Bend Wash Greenbelt for outdoor recreation, and the Chaparral Aquatic Center for swimming and fitness. Old Town Scottsdale and Scottsdale Fashion Square are minutes away for dining, shopping, and entertainment.

And the commute? The 101 freeway is right there. Whether you're heading to a job in Tempe, Phoenix, or Scottsdale's growing healthcare and tech corridors, 85250 puts you in a genuinely central location.

What Homes Look Like in the 85250 School Zone

The Chaparral Park area offers a mix of single-family homes, townhomes, and some condos across a variety of price points. Most 85250 homes sell between roughly $420K and $775K (median about $600K–$650K, as of October 2026), with condos and townhomes at the lower end and single-family homes at the upper end. For move-up buyers, you're typically looking at well-established neighborhoods with mature trees, generous lot sizes, and homes that have either been updated or offer strong renovation upside.

Investors and landlords are also active in the zip code specifically because SUSD zoning makes rental properties easier to fill and hold — families with kids aren't moving in and out every six months.
