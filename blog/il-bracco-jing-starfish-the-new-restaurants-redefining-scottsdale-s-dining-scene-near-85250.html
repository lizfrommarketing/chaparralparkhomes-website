Il Bracco, Jing & Starfish: The New Restaurants Redefining Scottsdale's Dining Scene Near 85250

Beyond Fashion Square: the newer Scottsdale restaurants within reach of 85250, and where each one actually sits relative to home.

Beyond the headline names at Fashion Square, Scottsdale's restaurant scene has been on a genuine growth tear — and several of the most talked-about new openings are within easy reach of the 85250 zip code. If you're considering a move to Chaparral Park or the surrounding area, here's what's new, what's worth knowing, and where each one actually sits relative to home.

Il Bracco: Texas's Most-Booked Restaurant Comes to Arizona

Il Bracco opened in 2026 at the corner of Scottsdale Road and Rose Lane, bordering Paradise Valley — making it roughly a 10 to 15 minute drive from most of the Chaparral Park area. The upscale Italian restaurant, originally from Dallas and Houston, was reportedly the most-booked restaurant in Dallas on OpenTable before expanding to Arizona, and it's brought that same energy west.

The 5,000-square-foot space features homemade pastas and breads, fresh fish, and prime meats butchered in-house daily, served in a richly designed dining room with leather, wood, travertine, and more than $100,000 in curated original artwork. Standout dishes include the Spicy Gemelli — a house-made pasta in vodka sauce — and the signature frozen Bracco cocktail, an Aperol and grapefruit blend that's become something of a calling card.

What stands out to me as a local agent is the restaurant's commitment to incorporating Arizona touches — Queen Creek Olive Mill olive oil and Cartel Roasting Co. espresso — into an otherwise Texas-to-Italy concept. It's the kind of detail that signals a restaurant group taking the Scottsdale market seriously, not just expanding for the sake of it.

Jing: Asian Fusion With a Nightlife Edge

Jing has built a following on N. Scottsdale Road for its sleek Asian-fusion concept that transitions naturally from dinner into a night out. The sushi and steak menu leans into shareable, social dining, with the wagyu and signature rolls earning consistent praise as reliable go-tos.

Jing sits further north along the Scottsdale Road corridor, making it more of a destination outing than a neighborhood spot for 85250 residents — but it's a relevant addition to the broader picture of how much Scottsdale's dining scene has expanded in recent years, and it's a regular mention when buyers ask what's new in the area.

Starfish: A Seafood Concept to Watch

Starfish is an upscale, seafood-forward concept that has been generating buzz in the Scottsdale dining conversation, part of the growing wave of destination dining that has made Scottsdale a genuine culinary city rather than just a resort town. I haven't been able to confirm a Scottsdale location or opening date, so I'm leaving specifics out rather than guess.

I'll update this post if details are confirmed — but it's a name to watch if seafood-focused fine dining is on your radar.

Why So Many New Restaurants Are Choosing Scottsdale Right Now

There's a reason national and out-of-state restaurant groups keep choosing Scottsdale for expansion: the demographics support it. Scottsdale has one of the highest concentrations of high-income households in the Phoenix metro, paired with a steady influx of relocating buyers from California, Texas, and the Midwest who are used to big-city dining and aren't willing to give it up.

For homeowners in 85250, this trend has a direct upside. Every new destination restaurant that opens nearby adds to the case for buying in this part of Scottsdale rather than further out in the suburbs, where this kind of culinary investment simply isn't happening at the same pace.

The Honest Take for Buyers

I'll be straight with you: not every new restaurant opening is walking distance from Chaparral Park. Some, like Jing and Il Bracco, are a short drive rather than a stroll. But the broader trend — Scottsdale becoming a legitimate culinary destination — benefits every neighborhood in its orbit, and 85250 sits closer to the center of that action than almost anywhere else in the city.

If you want a neighborhood where new restaurant openings are a regular occurrence rather than a rare event, this is it.
