How to Fix a Leaky Faucet: Single-Handle, Two-Handle and Tub Faucets, Step by Step for 85250 Homes

A faucet dripping once a second wastes more than 3,000 gallons a year, per EPA. How to find the worn washer, O-ring or cartridge, fix one- and two-handle faucets, and use Scottsdale's leak tools.

How do you fix a leaky faucet? Shut off the water under the sink, take the handle off, and replace the worn part inside: the cartridge or its O-rings in a single-handle faucet, or the rubber washer and seat in an older two-handle faucet. Bring the old part to the hardware store so the new one matches.

Why it's worth an hour: EPA WaterSense says a faucet dripping once per second can waste more than 3,000 gallons a year, about the water for more than 180 showers. EPA also notes that fixing easy household leaks can save about 10 percent on water bills.

Data note: Water-waste figures are from the EPA WaterSense Fix a Leak Week page. Scottsdale programs are from the Scottsdale Water rebates page. Both read November 23, 2026.

First, Find Where It Leaks

  • Drip from the spout with the handle off: worn cartridge, washer or seat
  • Water around the base of the spout: worn O-rings
  • Leak under the sink: a loose supply line or worn shutoff valve, not the faucet itself
  • Tub spout dripping: the valve cartridge or stem behind the handle

What You'll Need

  • Adjustable wrench and screwdrivers
  • Allen keys, since many handles use a hidden set screw
  • Replacement cartridge, washers or O-rings for your exact brand and model
  • Plumber's grease and white vinegar
  • A towel to cover the drain so small parts don't disappear

Step by Step: Single-Handle Faucet

  1. Turn off both shutoff valves under the sink, then open the faucet to drain pressure
  2. Pop off the decorative cap and remove the handle screw
  3. Remove the retaining nut or clip holding the cartridge
  4. Pull the cartridge straight out. Take a photo first so you know its orientation
  5. Inspect the O-rings and seals. Replace worn ones, or replace the whole cartridge
  6. Coat new O-rings with plumber's grease, reassemble and turn the water back on slowly

Step by Step: Two-Handle Faucet

  1. Shut off the water and open the faucet
  2. Remove the handle on the dripping side. Hot or cold? Feel the drip, or shut one valve at a time
  3. Unscrew the stem with a wrench
  4. Replace the rubber washer at the bottom of the stem and the O-ring on the stem
  5. Check the valve seat. If it's pitted, replace it or have a plumber reface it
  6. Reassemble and test

Newer two-handle faucets often use ceramic disc cartridges instead of washers. The steps are the same: remove, match and replace.

Tub and Shower Faucets

  • Shut off water at the home's main valve if there are no local stops
  • Remove the handle and trim plate to reach the cartridge or stem
  • If the valve is stuck or the stem is corroded, this is a good point to call a plumber

EPA adds that a showerhead leaking 10 drips per minute wastes more than 500 gallons a year, and that pipe tape on the connection often fixes it.

The Hard Water Factor in Scottsdale

Mineral buildup scratches seals and makes cartridges stick. When a part is out, soak it in white vinegar to dissolve scale before you decide to replace it. Clean the aerator at the tip of the spout the same way. More on that in does Scottsdale have hard water?

Use Scottsdale Water's Leak Tools

  • WaterSmart portal: customers with hourly meter data can receive leak notifications
  • Smart Home Water Monitor rebate: up to $100, with a deadline of April 30, 2027
  • Winter check: EPA suggests looking at use in a cold month. A family of four using more than 12,000 gallons a month may have serious leaks

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my faucet keep dripping?

Usually a worn washer, O-ring or cartridge, often sped up by hard water.

How much water does a leaky faucet waste?

More than 3,000 gallons a year at one drip per second, per EPA.

Can I fix a leaky faucet without replacing the cartridge?

Sometimes, with new O-rings and descaling. If the cartridge is worn, replace it.

When should I call a plumber for a leaky faucet?

If shutoffs don't work, the body is cracked or the leak continues after new parts.
