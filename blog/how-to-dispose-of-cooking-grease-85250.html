How to Dispose of Cooking Grease After Thanksgiving: Turkey Drippings, Fryer Oil and Bacon Fat, the Scottsdale 85250 Way

Never pour grease down the drain, even with hot water and soap. Scottsdale Water's method: cool it, pour it into a container with paper towels or coffee grounds, and trash it.

How do you dispose of cooking grease? Let it cool, pour it into a small container with something absorbent, like paper towels or used coffee grounds, and put it in the trash. That is Scottsdale Water's own advice to residents, and it applies to turkey drippings, bacon fat and the oil in your fryer alike.

What not to do: don't pour it down the sink, even chased with hot water and soap, and don't send it through the garbage disposal. The city says the grease simply cools and hardens farther down the line, where it clogs your pipes and the public sewer.

Data note: Guidance is from Scottsdale Water's residential fats, oils and grease (FOG) pages, its Maintenance, Prevention and Troubleshooting page, and the city's storm drain guidance. Read November 25, 2026. General information, not plumbing advice.

Why Grease and Drains Don't Mix

Scottsdale Water puts it plainly: fats, oils and grease from everyday cooking solidify in your pipes and can eventually cause blockages that send untreated wastewater backward, out of manholes or back into your house. The city lists the consequences:

  • Public health hazards from sewage overflows
  • Property damage and odor problems
  • Expensive repairs, often on the homeowner's side of the line

That last point matters in 85250. Scottsdale Water says the city maintains the sewer main, but the homeowner is responsible for the sewer lateral, the pipe that runs from the main to the house, including the tap that connects them. A grease clog in your lateral is your repair bill.

The Hot Water Myth

Most of us grew up running the hot tap after pouring out bacon fat. Scottsdale Water's answer:

  • Hot water and soap may wash grease out of sight, but not away
  • Hot grease cools and solidifies farther down your pipes
  • Once there, it can still clog sewer lines and cause backups in your home or street

How to Dispose of Cooking Grease, Step by Step

1. Let it cool

Hot oil burns, and it can soften or melt some plastic containers. A roasting pan cools fairly quickly on the stovetop; a full turkey fryer takes far longer.

2. Pour it into a container with absorbent material

Scottsdale Water recommends a small container with absorbent material, such as a paper towel or coffee grounds. An empty coffee can, a takeout tub with a lid, or a milk carton all work.

3. Trash it

Seal the container and put it in your regular trash, not recycling.

4. Wipe before you wash

The city recommends wiping greasy plates, pots and pans with a paper towel before they go in the sink or dishwasher, and scraping food into the trash, not the disposal.

5. Cover the drain

A sink strainer keeps food scraps, which carry grease with them, out of the line.

Turkey Fryer Oil: The Big-Batch Version

If you're frying a bird on Thursday, you'll have gallons of oil, not a few tablespoons. The same principles scale up:

  • Cool it completely, outdoors and covered, away from kids and pets
  • Strain it back into the original jugs if you plan to reuse it, or into several sealable containers with absorbent material if you're done with it
  • Never dump it on the ground, in the gravel, or down a street storm drain

Scottsdale's storm drain guidance is clear that storm drain water is not treated and flows directly into Indian Bend Wash, the greenbelt that runs right past Chaparral Park. Want the frying side of the job? Read how to deep fry a turkey safely.

Getting Rid of the Grease Smell

A searched follow-up question every Thanksgiving: the lingering smell. A few practical moves:

  • Run the range hood during and for a while after cooking
  • Wipe the cooktop, backsplash and hood filter; grease film holds odor
  • Take the trash out once your grease containers are in it
  • Open windows; late November in Scottsdale usually makes that easy

Garbage Disposals Aren't Grease Disposals

It's tempting to think the disposal handles everything. Scottsdale Water says disposals only grind greasy, fatty foods into smaller particles, which can make it even easier for grease to cling to pipes, and recommends limiting disposal use. Frequently Asked Questions

How do you dispose of cooking grease at home?

Cool it, pour it into a container with paper towels or coffee grounds, and trash it.

Can you pour grease down the drain with hot water and soap?

No. It hardens farther down the pipes, according to Scottsdale Water.

How do I get rid of turkey fryer oil?

Cool it fully, then contain it with absorbent material and trash it. Never use a storm drain.

Can grease go down the garbage disposal?

No. Disposals just make the grease particles smaller.
