Scottsdale Turkey Trot 2026: Date, Start Time, Parking and a Thanksgiving Morning Guide for 85250

Scottsdale Turkey Trot 2026: a Thanksgiving 5K on Nov. 26 at 8:30 a.m. at Embassy Suites in 85250. Entries, parking, weather and holiday trash pickup changes.

When is the Scottsdale Turkey Trot 2026? Thanksgiving morning, Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, with a 5K start at 8:30 a.m. MST. The race starts and finishes at Embassy Suites by Hilton Scottsdale Resort, 5001 N. Scottsdale Road, right here in ZIP 85250.

Is it just for runners? No. Organizers welcome walkers, joggers and stroller-pushers, every category is chip timed, and the finish turns into a wellness festival with music, food, a sauna and cold plunge. It's an easy Thanksgiving tradition to start close to home.

Data note: Event details come from the official Scottsdale Turkey Trot website and its Race Roster registration page, checked Oct. 29, 2026. Weather figures are NOAA NCEI 1991-2020 daily normals for Scottsdale Airport. Holiday schedules are from the City of Scottsdale. Event details can change; confirm with organizers before race day.

Scottsdale Turkey Trot 2026 at a Glance

  • Date: Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026 (Thanksgiving)
  • Start time: 8:30 a.m. MST
  • Start and finish: Embassy Suites by Hilton Scottsdale Resort, 5001 N. Scottsdale Road, Scottsdale, AZ 85250
  • Distance: 5K, described by organizers as flat and scenic, with Camelback Mountain views
  • Timing: chip timed, results posted after the race
  • Hosts: Scottsdale Run Club, Phoenix Run Club, Black AZ Run and Revival Wellness
  • Year: organizers bill 2026 as the event's second year

Entry Options

Organizers offer three categories on Race Roster:

  • General 5K: run, jog or walk, then stay for the festival
  • VIP 5K: upfront parking, separate check-in, an exclusive recovery area, pre-run breakfast bites, coffee and a gift bag
  • Kids 5K: for ages 12 and under, same course

Registration Details Worth Knowing

  • Pricing is shown on Race Roster for each category, with processing fees displayed before you pay
  • All sales are final; transfers are allowed until Nov. 7, 2026
  • Run clubs and teams can register through the "Run Clubs, Fitness Groups & Teams" link
  • Gift registrations are available
  • Packet pickup before race day is planned; details are to be announced on the race information page

Race Morning Tips

Parking

Complimentary parking at Embassy Suites is limited and first come, first served. VIP entries include upfront parking. If you live nearby, walking or biking over is worth considering.

What to Wear

NOAA's 1991-2020 daily normals for Scottsdale Airport put Nov. 26 at an average high of 69.6°F and low of 46.4°F. An 8:30 a.m. start will feel cool, so:

  • Wear a light layer you can shed
  • Bring sunglasses and sunscreen; the desert sun is strong even in late November
  • Carry water for the walk to and from your car

Rules to Remember

  • Dogs stay home
  • Strollers are welcome
  • Allow time to park and reach the start line

After the Finish

The finish line becomes a wellness festival with a live DJ, sauna, cold plunge, recovery area, food, local vendors and arts and crafts. Plan an extra hour before the turkey goes in.

Thanksgiving Week Logistics in 85250

Trash and Recycling

Per the City of Scottsdale's 2026 holiday schedule:

  • No collection on Thanksgiving Day
  • Thursday collection moves to Friday
  • Friday collection moves to Saturday
  • City offices are closed Nov. 26 and 27
