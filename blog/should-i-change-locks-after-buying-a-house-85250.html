Should I Change the Locks After Buying a House? A First-Week Security Checklist for Scottsdale Buyers

Yes, rekey or change the locks after buying a house. What Arizona contracts say sellers must hand over, plus a first-week security checklist for 85250 buyers.

Should you change the locks after buying a house? Yes. You can't know how many keys are out there. Sellers, past tenants, cleaners, contractors and neighbors may all have had one. Rekeying or replacing every exterior lock right after closing is the simple way to be sure.

What Arizona contracts cover: the Phoenix REALTORS resale purchase contract says the seller "shall provide all existing keys, security system/alarm codes, gate openers and garage door openers to Buyer at possession." That covers what the seller hands over, not copies made over the years. Here's the full first-week checklist.

Data note: Contract language is from the Phoenix REALTORS example Residential Resale Purchase Contract (Dec. 2024), lines 50-52. Garage opener steps are from LiftMaster's Security+ remote control manual. Contractor licensing is from A.R.S. § 32-1121. Read November 10, 2026.

Quick Answers

  • Change or rekey? Rekey if the hardware is in good shape; replace if it's worn, damaged or you want an upgrade
  • Which doors: front, back, side, garage-to-house door, and any side-yard gate with a lock
  • Garage openers: erase old remote codes and reset the keypad PIN
  • Codes: reset alarm, smart lock, video doorbell and keypad codes
  • When: the day you get possession, before you move belongings in

Rekey vs. Replace

Rekeying

A locksmith swaps the pins inside the existing cylinder so the old keys no longer work. You keep your hardware and get new keys. Rekeying is usually the lower-cost route, especially if several doors can be set to one key.

Replacing

Replace when:

  • A lock sticks, wobbles or shows forced-entry damage
  • You want new hardware that matches a remodel
  • You're switching to a smart lock or keypad deadbolt

Hiring in Arizona

Under A.R.S. § 32-1121, the handyman exemption from Arizona contractor licensing covers minor work with an aggregate price, including labor and materials, under $1,000, and it doesn't apply when a building permit is required. Larger jobs, such as a full door and lock replacement across the house, may call for a licensed contractor. Confirm any license on the Arizona Registrar of Contractors site.

Your First-Week Security Checklist

Day one: locks and keys

  • Rekey or replace every exterior door lock, including the garage-to-house door
  • Don't forget side gates, sheds, mailbox locks and any pool gate padlocks
  • Make a short list of who now has your new keys

Garage door openers

Old remotes may still be in a former owner's car. LiftMaster's Security+ manual says to press and hold the Learn button on the opener until the Learn LED goes out (approximately 6 seconds), which erases all previous codes. Then reprogram each remote you plan to use. Change the exterior keypad PIN too.

Codes and connected devices

  • Alarm system: have the provider transfer the account and issue new master and user codes
  • Smart locks: factory-reset and remove the previous owner's app access
  • Video doorbells and cameras: confirm the seller removed them from their account
  • Wi-Fi thermostats and irrigation controllers: reset and re-register in your name

HOA and community access

If your street has gate remotes, a pool fob or community facility keys, contact the HOA or management company to deactivate the seller's credentials and issue yours.

What Stays, What Goes

The contract's keys-and-openers clause sits alongside the list of fixtures that convey with the home. For more on what's included, see is everything left in the house yours. New owners should also consider the county's free title alert; our Arizona deed fraud guide explains how to sign up.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I change the locks after buying a house?

Yes. Rekey or replace every exterior lock, since you can't account for every copy.

What does the seller have to give me at closing in Arizona?

Under the Phoenix REALTORS contract: all existing keys, alarm codes, gate openers and garage door openers at possession.

Is it cheaper to rekey or replace locks?

Rekeying is usually less expensive because you keep the hardware. Get quotes.

How do I reset a garage door opener after buying a house?

On many LiftMaster/Chamberlain units, hold the Learn button about 6 seconds, then reprogram your remotes.
