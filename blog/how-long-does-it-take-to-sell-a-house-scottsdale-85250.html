How Long Does It Take to Sell a House in Scottsdale? A Realistic 85250 Timeline From Listing to Keys

Scottsdale homes took 75 days on average to reach an accepted offer in October 2026, then a typical loan needs about five more weeks to close. The full 85250 timeline.

How long does it take to sell a house in Scottsdale? Plan on about three to four months from listing to closing. Phoenix REALTORS' October 2026 report shows Scottsdale single-family homes averaged 75 days on market until sale, meaning listing to accepted offer. A financed buyer then needs time to close: ICE found the average U.S. purchase loan closed in 36.8 days in March 2026.

The short version for 85250 sellers: roughly 11 weeks to an accepted offer plus about five weeks of escrow, before counting any prep time. Pricing, condition and timing can move both numbers. Here's how the calendar actually breaks down.

Data note: Market figures are from the Phoenix REALTORS/ShowingTime Local Market Update for Scottsdale, October 2026 (current as of Nov. 3, 2026; ARMLS data). The days-on-market definition is from Phoenix REALTORS' Housing Supply Overview. Closing-time data are national figures from ICE's May 2026 Mortgage Monitor. Contract terms are from the Phoenix REALTORS example Residential Resale Purchase Contract (Dec. 2024). Read November 11, 2026.

Scottsdale Selling Speed at a Glance (October 2026)

Single-family homes:

  • Days on market until sale: 75 (down from 82 in October 2025)
  • Year-to-date average: 82 days
  • Median sales price: $1,225,000
  • Percent of list price received: 96.8%
  • Months supply of inventory: 4.2

Townhouses and condos:

  • Days on market until sale: 78 (year to date: 94)
  • Median sales price: $485,000
  • Months supply of inventory: 6.6

What "days on market" means: Phoenix REALTORS defines it as the average number of days between listing and an accepted offer. Escrow comes after.

The Three Phases of a Scottsdale Sale

Phase 1: Getting ready (before day one)

This is the part you control. Some sellers list within a week; others take a month for paint, repairs and photography. A pre-listing inspection and a smart staging plan take time up front but can save days later.

Phase 2: On the market (about 75 days on average)

Averages hide a wide range. Well-priced, well-presented homes often go under contract well ahead of the average; overpriced homes sit and usually end up reducing. With Scottsdale sellers receiving 96.8% of list price on average, buyers are negotiating. Getting the price right on day one is the single biggest lever on this phase. Our guide to how to price your house walks through it.

Phase 3: Escrow (about five weeks with a loan)

Once you accept an offer, the Phoenix REALTORS contract sets the pace:

  • Close of escrow must be a specific date written into the contract
  • Seller's disclosure (SPDS): due within 3 days after acceptance
  • Inspection period: the form's residential option is 10 days
  • Repair negotiations: the buyer sends one signed notice of objections before the inspection period ends; the seller has 5 days to respond
  • Appraisal contingency: if the home appraises low, the buyer has 5 days after notice to cancel

Nationally, ICE measured 11 days from loan application to rate lock and 26 more to closing. Cash buyers skip loan underwriting and can often close faster.

Putting It Together: A Sample Timeline

Using October's averages (arithmetic, not a promise):

  • Weeks 1–2: prep, photos and listing launch
  • Weeks 2–13: about 75 days on market to an accepted offer
  • Weeks 13–18: about 37 days of escrow with a financed buyer
  • Total: roughly 16–18 weeks, or about four months

If you list this month, that math points to a spring closing. If you need to be out sooner, price and condition matter more than anything else.

What Speeds Up (or Slows Down) a Sale

Faster:

  • Pricing in line with recent comparable sales
  • Clean inspections, or known issues disclosed and priced in
  • Flexible showing access
  • Offering a closing date that fits the buyer

Slower:

  • Pricing above the market and waiting for it to catch up
  • Deferred maintenance that turns up in inspection
  • Limited showing windows
  • Condos with higher inventory (6.6 months of supply in October)

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to sell a house in Scottsdale right now?

About three to four months: 75 days on average to an accepted offer in October 2026, plus roughly five weeks to close with a loan.

What does days on market mean?

The average days from listing to an accepted offer, per Phoenix REALTORS. It doesn't include escrow.

How long does it take to sell a house from start to finish?

Prep time, plus about 75 days on market, plus about 37 days of escrow with a financed buyer.
