What Is the Median Home Price in Scottsdale? October 2026 Numbers for the City, ZIP 85250 and Condos

Scottsdale's median single-family sales price was $1,225,000 in October 2026, up 5.6% from a year earlier. Average price, condo median and ZIP 85250 numbers explained.

What is the median home price in Scottsdale? The median single-family sales price in Scottsdale was $1,225,000 in October 2026, up 5.6% from a year earlier, according to Phoenix REALTORS®. The townhouse and condo median was $485,000.

Closer to home: in ZIP 85250, which includes Chaparral Park, the October single-family median was $825,000 and the year-to-date median was $865,000.

Data note: All figures are from Phoenix REALTORS® Local Market Updates for Scottsdale, ZIP 85250, Phoenix and Paradise Valley, October 2026, the latest published. Phoenix REALTORS® notes that prices don't account for seller concessions or down payment assistance, and that small samples can produce extreme percent changes. Read November 26, 2026.

Median vs. Average: Which Number to Use

People search both, and they tell different stories.

  • The median is the middle sale. Half of homes sold for more, half for less. It's the better "typical home" number
  • The average adds every sale and divides. In Scottsdale, a small number of very expensive sales pull it up, which is why October's average, $1,618,450, ran about $393,000 above the median

When a headline says Scottsdale homes "average" $1.6 million, it's describing the top end as much as the middle.

Read it carefully: October's single-family median came from just 13 sales, so one or two big or small closings move it. The year-to-date figure, built on 135 sales, is the steadier signal, and it's up.

What Moves the Scottsdale Median

A monthly median isn't a price index. Several things push it around:

  • The mix of sales: each month's median reflects whichever homes happened to close. More North Scottsdale estate closings lift it; more central Scottsdale ranch homes or condos pull it down
  • Concessions aren't netted out: Phoenix REALTORS® notes its prices don't account for seller concessions or down payment assistance, so a sale with a large seller credit still counts at its full price
  • Small samples swing: a ZIP code with a dozen sales can post double-digit moves in either direction
  • Pending sales lead closings: Scottsdale had 208 pending single-family sales in October, down from 368 a year earlier, which points to fewer closings in the weeks ahead

That's why year-to-date figures, built on thousands of sales, are the better gauge of direction.

How Scottsdale Compares

Single-family medians, October 2026:

  • Paradise Valley: $5,300,000

  • ZIP 85250: $825,000

85250 sits in a useful middle: central Scottsdale, the Indian Bend Wash greenbelt and Old Town nearby, at roughly two-thirds of the citywide median.
