Should I Take My House Off the Market Over Christmas? What 85250 Sellers Should Weigh Before the Holidays

Should you take your house off the market over Christmas? How Temp Off Market works in ARMLS, what it does to days on market, and what October 2026 data says for 85250.

Should you take your house off the market over Christmas? Usually not for the whole season. In 85250, a better move is to stay active and block out the specific days you're hosting, or use Temp Off Market for a short, defined window. ARMLS says Temp Off Market pauses days on market instead of resetting it, so a long break doesn't buy a fresh start.

The local picture: in October 2026, ZIP 85250 single-family homes averaged 89 days on market, and only 15 new listings came on, down from 27 a year earlier. Fewer listings means less competition for the buyers who are looking.

Data note: MLS status rules are from ARMLS, the Arizona Regional Multiple Listing Service. Market figures are from the Phoenix REALTORS® Local Market Update for ZIP 85250 and Scottsdale, October 2026 (single-family), the latest published. Read November 26, 2026. Your Three Options

1. Stay Active, Limit Showings

ARMLS notes that showings are always the seller's decision, in any status. You can stay on the market and decline showings Dec. 24 to 26, for example, without changing anything in the MLS.

  • Best for: sellers who want to keep exposure and catch serious holiday buyers
  • Trade-off: days on market keep counting

2. Temp Off Market (TOM)

  • What it does: removes the listing from active status. ARMLS says it pauses days on market, and the count picks up where it left off when you return
  • How long: ARMLS sets no maximum, but if your listing's expiration date passes, it automatically becomes Expired
  • Signs and lockboxes: ARMLS says the lockbox can stay or go during TOM, and the sign doesn't have to come down if you've given permission
  • Best for: a defined break, like a two-week trip or a house full of relatives

3. Cancel and Relist Later

Cancelling ends the active MLS listing. Whether you can cancel, and on what terms, depends on your listing agreement with your brokerage, so read it and talk with your agent first.

What the October Numbers Say for 85250

Phoenix REALTORS® data, single-family, October 2026 vs. October 2025:

  • New listings: 15, down from 27
  • Pending sales: 4, down from 16
  • Closed sales: 13, down from 16
  • Days on market: 89, up from 80
  • Median sales price: $825,000, down 7.2%
  • Percent of list price received: 96.8%
  • Inventory: 51 homes, 4.2 months of supply

Citywide, Scottsdale had 523 new single-family listings in October, down from 609, with 1,480 homes for sale and 4.2 months of supply.

What that means: buyers are slower to commit, but fewer sellers are competing for them. A listing that stays visible through December can stand out in a thin field.

Reasons to Stay on the Market

  • Less competition: new listings in 85250 fell 44.4% year over year in October
  • Motivated buyers: people touring in December often have a reason, like a job start or a lease ending
  • Holiday staging is easy: a lit tree and a warm entry photograph well. Our staging guide covers the rest
  • Momentum: a pause doesn't reset days on market, so pulling the listing doesn't fix a high count

Reasons to Step Back Briefly

  • You'll be traveling and can't keep the home show-ready
  • The house is full and showings would disrupt the holiday
  • You plan to refresh photos, price or condition for a January relaunch

If you go TOM, set the return date before you leave and confirm it with your agent. For the bigger timing question, see should I sell my house in the winter?

A Holiday Showing Plan

If you stay active, a little structure keeps the season manageable:

  • Block dates in advance: tell your agent which days are off-limits, so those days can be blocked on the showing schedule
  • Keep a 15-minute reset kit: a basket for clutter, a quick wipe-down and lights on
  • Decorate lightly: one tree and a wreath photograph better than every surface covered
  • Secure gifts and valuables before showings, as you would any time
  • Refresh photos if your listing shows summer landscaping or empty rooms
“In 85250, December buyers are real buyers. I usually suggest staying active and blocking the two or three days that matter to your family, rather than disappearing for a month. If you do need a break, Temp Off Market is cleaner than cancelling, just come back with fresh photos and a clear plan.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Does taking a house off the market reset days on market?

Not with Temp Off Market in ARMLS. It pauses the count; it doesn't reset it.

Can I take my house off the market at any time?

Your agent can change the status with your permission. Your listing agreement still applies.

Do houses sell during the holidays?

Yes, more slowly. 85250 closed 13 single-family sales in October.

Should I stop showings without taking my house off the market?

You can. Showings are always the seller's decision.
