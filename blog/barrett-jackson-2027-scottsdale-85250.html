Barrett-Jackson 2027 Scottsdale: Dates, Tickets, Family Day, Parking and What's New at the Nine-Day Auction

Barrett-Jackson's 2027 Scottsdale Auction runs nine days, Jan. 23-31, at WestWorld, with Family Day on Jan. 23. Dates, tickets, parking, bag rules and tips for 85250 locals.

When is Barrett-Jackson Scottsdale 2027? The 2027 Barrett-Jackson Scottsdale Auction runs Saturday, Jan. 23 through Sunday, Jan. 31, 2027, at WestWorld of Scottsdale, 16601 N. Pima Road. For the first time, the flagship auction stretches across nine days and two full weekends, with well over 2,000 collector vehicles expected, according to Barrett-Jackson.

The best day for families: opening day, Saturday, Jan. 23, is Family Day, with free admission for kids 12 and under and discounts for students, seniors, military personnel and first responders. Here's what 85250 locals need to know before heading to WestWorld.

Data note: Dates, schedule, ticket, parking and venue policies are from Barrett-Jackson's 2027 Scottsdale event page and its Sept. 16 and Oct. 6, 2026 press releases. Read November 12, 2026. Details can change; confirm with Barrett-Jackson before you go.

Barrett-Jackson 2027 at a Glance

  • Dates: Jan. 23-31, 2027 (nine days)
  • Opening Night Gala: Friday, Jan. 22, 7-10 p.m., by invitation only
  • Venue: WestWorld of Scottsdale, 16601 N. Pima Road, Scottsdale 85260
  • Gates, food courts and sponsor displays: open 8 a.m. to auction close on auction days
  • Docket: Barrett-Jackson aims for well over 2,000 collectible vehicles plus an expanded automobilia auction
  • Family Day: Saturday, Jan. 23, presented by Coca-Cola, with STEM Fest programming
  • Payments: the event is cashless; credit and debit cards accepted

What's New for 2027

Nine days instead of one long week

Barrett-Jackson announced in September that it is opening the auction block a full weekend earlier to handle demand from consignors. That means two Saturdays and two Sundays of auction action, which makes it easier to plan a visit around work.

Early headliners

The October lineup announcement highlighted a 2019 Ford GT Heritage Edition, a custom 1968 Ford Mustang fastback known as "The Outlaw," a 1996 Vector M12, a 2006 Maybach 57 formerly owned by Elizabeth Taylor, and the last Toyota GR Supra MkV Final Edition, with its entire hammer price benefiting Reach Out WorldWide. The full docket is listed as coming soon.

The Seventy-One Club

A new premium venue above the Bidders Bar overlooks the auction arena, with admission and hosted bar included. Access is limited.

Tickets and Seating

  • General admission is sold online and on-site at the AXS box office
  • A general admission ticket does not guarantee a seat in the auction arena; spectators come and go, so seats open up through the day, and big-screen seating areas sit next to the arena
  • Floor, skybox and Muscle Lounge seating is reserved for bidders, consignors and credentialed guests
  • Family Day (Jan. 23): free for children 12 and under; discounted tickets for students, seniors, military and first responders

Check Barrett-Jackson's ticket page for current daily prices before you buy.

Getting There From 85250

WestWorld sits off Pima Road and the Loop 101 in north Scottsdale. From the Chaparral Park area, give yourself extra time on the two weekends.

  • Parking: Barrett-Jackson says there is plenty of complimentary public parking; follow the signs, and shuttles run to the main gate
  • Valet: available for a fee
  • Rideshare: use the lot west of Thompson Peak Parkway on McDowell Mountain Ranch Road
  • Golf carts and scooters: rentable through the vendors listed on the event page; carts aren't allowed indoors or in vehicle tents

Know Before You Go

  • Clear bag policy: clutches up to 6.5 x 4.5 inches or clear bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches; all bags are checked and guests are screened
  • Water: one sealed bottle per person under 17 oz.; other outside food and drinks are prohibited
  • Not allowed: weapons, video cameras, skateboards, drones, coolers and animals (licensed service animals are allowed)
  • Thrill Rides and Hot Laps: daily, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., for guests age 8 or 48 inches tall and up
  • Weather: most of the site is climate-controlled; the outdoor performance track isn't. Late-January evenings can be cool, so bring a layer. Our guide to Scottsdale weather in December is a good primer on winter nights.

Hosting Out-of-Town Guests?

Late January and early February bring back-to-back draws: nine days of Barrett-Jackson, then the WM Phoenix Open at TPC Scottsdale, Feb. 8-14, 2027. If you're thinking about renting out a casita or a second property for event season, review the city's short-term rental rules first. And if you're adding space for guests, our Scottsdale casita and ADU guide covers the basics.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Barrett-Jackson Scottsdale 2027?

Jan. 23-31, 2027, at WestWorld of Scottsdale, with an invitation-only gala on Jan. 22.

How much are Barrett-Jackson 2027 tickets?

General admission is sold online and at the AXS box office; kids 12 and under are free on Family Day. Check Barrett-Jackson's site for daily prices.
