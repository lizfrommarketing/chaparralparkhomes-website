How to Light a Gas Fireplace (and Keep the Glass Clean): A Winter Guide for Scottsdale and 85250 Homes

How to light a gas fireplace with a switch, a pilot knob or a key valve, how to clean the glass safely, and why gas still works on Maricopa County No Burn Days.

How do you light a gas fireplace? It depends on which of three setups you have: an electronic ignition that starts from a wall switch or remote, a standing pilot with an OFF/PILOT/ON knob, or a key valve that feeds gas logs in a wood-burning firebox. In every case, the lighting label or owner's manual for your model is the final word, and if you smell gas, don't light anything.

Why it matters now: gas is the fuel Maricopa County still allows on No Burn Days, and glass-front gas units get hot enough to burn skin in an instant. A CPSC chairman's statement described the glass on these fireplaces reaching 500 to 1,000 degrees. Here's how to light yours, clean it and use it safely in an 85250 home.

Data note: Safety standards are from the Hearth, Patio & Barbecue Association and HPBA Canada, the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission's 2012 statement on glass-front gas fireplaces, and Maricopa County Air Quality's residential burning rules. Read November 13, 2026.

First, Identify Your Fireplace

  • Electronic ignition: a wall switch, thermostat or remote; no standing flame when off
  • Standing pilot: a small, always-on blue flame and a control knob marked OFF / PILOT / ON, usually behind a lower grille
  • Key valve: a square key socket in the wall or floor near a wood-burning fireplace with gas logs or a log lighter
  • Glass front: most sealed direct-vent units have one; key-valve log sets usually don't

Not sure? The rating plate (often near the valve) lists the model number, and most manufacturers post manuals online.

How to Light a Gas Fireplace, Step by Step

Electronic ignition (switch or remote)

  • Replace remote batteries first; dead batteries are the most common "it won't light"
  • Flip the switch or press ON and give it a few seconds
  • If it clicks but won't light, stop and check the manual's troubleshooting section

Standing pilot with a control knob

  • Turn the knob to OFF and wait the time your label specifies so any gas can clear
  • Turn to PILOT, then press and hold the knob
  • Click the igniter button until the pilot lights
  • Keep holding for the time the manual specifies so the thermocouple warms up, then release and turn to ON
  • Use the wall switch or remote to start the main burner

Key valve (gas logs in a wood-burning fireplace)

  • Open the damper first
  • Hold a long lighter at the burner before you open the gas
  • Open the key slowly, then adjust the flame
  • Close the key fully when you're done, and close the damper only once everything is cold

If you smell gas: don't light anything or flip switches. Leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 from outside.

How to Clean Gas Fireplace Glass

That white haze is normal, especially in the first weeks of the season.

  • Let it cool completely. HPBA Canada warns that glass can stay extremely hot long after the appliance is off
  • Remove the glass only as the manual describes; many units use clips or latches
  • Use a cleaner your manufacturer approves for fireplace glass
  • Never run the unit with cracked or broken glass
  • Book a professional inspection at the interval your manufacturer recommends; HPBA Canada advises a licensed gas contractor for cleaning and inspection

The Hot-Glass Rule Every Household Should Know

  • Since Jan. 1, 2015, newly manufactured glass-front gas fireplaces whose glass exceeds 172 degrees Fahrenheit must include a protective barrier, per the HPBA
  • Older units may not have one; ask the manufacturer for a barrier approved for your model
  • Metal frames and grilles get hot too
  • Keep remotes out of reach of young children and consider a switch lock

Gas vs. Wood on a No Burn Day

On a Maricopa County No Burn Day, only gaseous fuels, including gas logs, are allowed in fireplaces and fire pits. Wood fires wait until the next clear day. See our full No Burn Day guide for the rules and fines.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you light a gas fireplace with a pilot knob?

Turn to PILOT, press and hold, click the igniter, hold as the manual specifies, release, then turn to ON. If it won't stay lit, call a gas technician.

How do you light a gas fireplace with a key?

Open the damper, hold a long lighter at the burner, then open the key slowly. Never open the gas first.

How do you clean gas fireplace glass?

Let it cool completely, remove it as the manual describes and use a manufacturer-approved cleaner.

Can I use my gas fireplace on a No Burn Day in Maricopa County?

Yes. Gaseous fuels, including gas logs, are allowed. Wood fires are not.
