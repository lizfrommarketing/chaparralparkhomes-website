How Much Below Asking Price Should You Offer on a House in Scottsdale 85250? October 2026 Data

How much below asking should you offer in 85250? Homes sold for 96.73% of list on average (Cromford, 10/22/26), about 3.3% under. What that means for your offer.

How much below asking do 85250 homes sell for? According to The Cromford Report (ZIP 85250 single-family snapshot, measured Oct. 22, 2026), homes sold for an average of 96.73% of list price, about 3.3% under asking. A year ago that figure was 97.08%.

So should you offer 3% under? Use that as context, not a formula. The right number depends on how long the home has been listed, how its price compares with recent sales, and how much competition it's drawing. With a median of 58 days to first contract, buyers have more room on listings that have sat than they did a year ago.

Data note: All figures are from The Cromford Report ZIP 85250 snapshot (single-family detached), "Latest - 10/22/26," with comparisons to 9/22/26 and 10/22/25. Monthly figures rest on small samples (last month's months-of-supply reading of 10.8 reflects just 4 sales), so treat single-month swings with caution.

85250 Sale-to-List Price: The Numbers

Average sale price as % of list (Cromford, 85250 single-family):

  • Today (10/22/26): 96.73%
  • Last month (9/22/26): 99.08%
  • Last year (10/22/25): 97.08%
  • Two years ago (10/22/24): 96.17%

What it means in dollars: as a simple illustration, 96.73% of an $870,000 list price is about $841,600, roughly $28,400 under asking. Your actual gap will depend on the specific home.

The Market Behind the Discount

Demand Is Slower to Show Up

  • Median days to first contract: 58, vs. 18 a year ago
  • Median days on market, active listings: 66
  • Contract ratio: 23.9, down from 35.6 a year ago
  • Listings under contract: 11, vs. 16 a year ago

Supply Is Steady

  • Active listings: 49 (50 a year ago)
  • Months of supply (excl. UCB): 3.1, vs. 3.8 a year ago
  • Days of inventory (excl. UCB): 106

Prices Over 12 Months Are Still Up

  • Median sale price (annual): $870,000, up 8.3%
  • Average sale price (annual): $1,257,078
  • Median sales $/SF (annual): $454.55, up 4.1%
  • Median active listing $/SF: $476.42; under contract: $479.21

Deals Do Fall Apart

  • Annual fall-through rate: 19.9%
  • Listing success rate: 71.4%

The takeaway: 12-month prices in 85250 are higher than a year ago, but today's listings are waiting longer for a buyer. That combination is when negotiation tends to be listing-specific: fresh, well-priced homes can still command close to asking, while homes that have sat for weeks often have more give.

How to Decide What to Offer

Signals You Have More Room

  • Days on market well past the 85250 median
  • Price reductions already taken
  • Asking $/SF above recent comparable sales
  • Deferred maintenance an inspection is likely to confirm

Signals to Offer Closer to Asking

  • New listing priced in line with comps
  • Multiple showings or an offer deadline
  • Updated systems: roof, HVAC, plumbing

Negotiate Beyond Price

  • Seller concessions toward closing costs or a rate buydown (see our concessions and buydowns guide)
  • Repairs through the inspection period
  • Closing timeline that suits the seller

What Sellers Should Take From This

  • Price to the market on day one. Median active listings ask $476.42/SF, while the 12-month median sale came in at $454.55/SF
  • Expect negotiation. An average sale at 96.73% of list means buyers will likely ask for something
  • Watch the clock. With 58 days to first contract, a home that is still waiting at the two-month mark is a candidate for a price review
  • Read monthly numbers carefully. The latest monthly median sales price per square foot was $513.65, but it rests on just 15 sales
