Things to Do in Scottsdale in December 2026: Holiday Lights, Markets, Ice Skating and Shows Near 85250

Scottsdale's December 2026 calendar, verified: Holiday Lights at McCormick-Stillman (in 85250), Scottsdazzle, Christmas at the Princess, the Merry Merchantile Market and more.

What is there to do in Scottsdale in December 2026? Start with Holiday Lights at McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park (Nov. 27 to Dec. 30, right inside 85250), Scottsdazzle across Old Town (Nov. 28 to Dec. 31), Christmas at the Princess (through Jan. 2) and the free Merry Merchantile Market at Scottsdale Civic Center on Dec. 12 and 13.

For Chaparral Park neighbors, most of the month's best outings are a short drive, and several are free. Here's the verified calendar, in the order you'll want to plan it.

Data note: Dates, hours and prices are from each organizer's official page: McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park, Scottsdazzle (City of Scottsdale Tourism & Events), Christmas at the Princess, The Merchantile Market, Phoenix Zoo, Desert Botanical Garden and Ballet Arizona. Read November 19, 2026. Events can change, so confirm before you go.

In Your Backyard: Holiday Lights at McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park

The park sits at 7301 E. Indian Bend Rd., Scottsdale, AZ 85250, which makes this the closest big holiday event to Chaparral Park.

  • Dates: November 27 through December 30, 2026; no Holiday Lights on Dec. 24 or 25
  • Hours: 5:30 to 9:30 p.m.
  • Tickets: $15 per person, ages 3 and older (wristbands required for entry and rides)
  • Santa & Friends: meet-and-greets on Dec. 1, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17 and 19 through 23
  • Heads-up: the park says the event sells out every year

More detail in our Holiday Lights guide.

Old Town: Scottsdazzle (Nov. 28 to Dec. 31)

The City of Scottsdale's month-long celebration runs November 28 through December 31. Two December highlights, both free:

  • Winter at the Waterfront: Saturday, Dec. 5, 6 to 9 p.m., Scottsdale Waterfront Canal Bank, with live music and pop-up boutiques along Stetson Plaza
  • Starlight Movie Night: Saturday, Dec. 5, 7 to 9 p.m., showing Elf

The city notes parking in Old Town is free, with time limits on the street. Full calendar in our Scottsdazzle 2026 guide.

Holiday Shopping: Merry Merchantile Market (Dec. 12 and 13)

  • Where: Scottsdale Civic Center, 3939 N. Drinkwater Blvd.
  • When: Saturday, Dec. 12, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.; Sunday, Dec. 13, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.
  • Cost: free admission
  • What's there: 150+ local vendors, free live concerts and Santa photos from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. both days

Other markets are in our Scottsdale Christmas markets roundup.

Big Lights: Princess, ZooLights and the Garden

Christmas at the Princess

  • Dates: November 20, 2026 through January 2, 2027
  • Hours: Monday to Thursday 5 to 10 p.m., Friday to Sunday 4 to 11 p.m.; beginning Dec. 16, daily 4 to 11 p.m.
  • Santa: every night through Dec. 24

Phoenix ZooLights

  • Dates: November 25, 2026 through January 15, 2027, 5:30 to 10:30 p.m.
  • General admission: $35
  • Crowd tip: the zoo says the first two weeks of December tend to be less popular; there's a sensory-friendly night on Tuesday, Dec. 8

Las Noches de las Luminarias at Desert Botanical Garden

  • December nights: Dec. 4 to 6, 11 to 13, 16 to 23 and 26 to 31, 5 to 9:30 p.m.

On Stage: The Nutcracker

Ballet Arizona's The Nutcracker runs December 11 to 27, 2026 at Symphony Hall in downtown Phoenix, with live music by The Phoenix Symphony. The company lists runtime at about two hours with one intermission and no late seating.

What to Pack

December means mild afternoons and cool nights. Our Scottsdale weather in December guide has the averages; for evening light shows, bring a real jacket.

Frequently Asked Questions

What events are in Scottsdale in December 2026?

Scottsdazzle, Holiday Lights at McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park, Christmas at the Princess and the Merry Merchantile Market lead the list.

What is there to do in Scottsdale in December with kids?

Railroad park lights, Starlight Movie Night (Elf, Dec. 5), Princess ice skating and market Santa photos.

What free things are there to do in Scottsdale in December?

Winter at the Waterfront, Starlight Movie Night and the Merry Merchantile Market.

Can you swim in Scottsdale in December?

Only in a heated pool for most people; December highs average about 66°F.
