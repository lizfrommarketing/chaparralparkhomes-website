Canal Convergence 2026 in Scottsdale: Dates, Hours, Theme and How to Plan Your Night From 85250

When is Canal Convergence 2026? Nov. 6-15 at the Scottsdale Waterfront, free, with nightly hours of 6 to 9 or 10 p.m. Here's the theme, schedule and a plan from 85250.

When is Canal Convergence 2026? Scottsdale Arts' light-art festival runs Friday, Nov. 6, through Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, at the Scottsdale Waterfront. It's free, it lasts 10 nights, and hours are 6 to 10 p.m. on the two Friday-Saturday pairs and 6 to 9 p.m. on every other night, including closing night.

What's different this year? The 2026 theme is "Western Nature," and it's the event's 15th year, landing during the City of Scottsdale's 75th anniversary. From Chaparral Park, the Waterfront is a short drive across town, which makes this one of the easiest big events of the season to fit into a weeknight.

Data note: Dates, hours, location and program details come from Scottsdale Arts' Canal Convergence 2026 event page, its Plan Your Visit page and its June 24, 2026 announcement. Transit hours come from the City of Scottsdale. No market statistics are included in this post.

Canal Convergence 2026 at a Glance

  • Dates: Nov. 6-15, 2026 (10 nights)
  • Cost: free to attend
  • Where: the Scottsdale Waterfront, along the Arizona Canal between Scottsdale Road and Goldwater Boulevard
  • Address listed: Solstice Park, 4420 N. Scottsdale Road, Scottsdale 85251
  • Theme: Western Nature
  • Highlights: large-scale light art, nightly fire shows, live music and dance, workshops, food trucks and a beer and wine garden

Hours, Night by Night

The Longer Nights

  • Friday, Nov. 6, and Saturday, Nov. 7: 6-10 p.m.
  • Friday, Nov. 13, and Saturday, Nov. 14: 6-10 p.m.

The Earlier Nights

  • Sunday, Nov. 8, through Thursday, Nov. 12: 6-9 p.m.
  • Sunday, Nov. 15 (closing night): 6-9 p.m.

If you'd like more room to linger at each installation, the Sunday-through-Thursday stretch is the one to circle. Weekends bring the fullest energy and the most people on the bridges.

What "Western Nature" Means This Year

Scottsdale Arts says artists were invited to explore the environmental and cultural landscapes of the American West, from water resources, natural landscapes, climate and native flora and fauna to the region's cultures and histories.

Names to Look For

  • Walter Productions, the Arizona team behind the fan-favorite fire shows, returns with a desert flora and fauna focus
  • Ottomata, an experiential design studio
  • Jay Tettamanti of Chalk River Studio, based in Australia
  • Curious Form, the art and fabrication studio led by William Nemitoff

Those names come from the finalist list in Scottsdale Arts' June announcement. The organization said the full artist list and daily schedules would follow on the event site, so check it the afternoon you go.

How to Plan Your Night From Chaparral Park

Getting There

Driving or a rideshare is the simplest call for an evening visit. Scottsdale's fare-free trolley runs Monday through Friday, 5:45 a.m. to 7:45 p.m., so it can get you to Old Town for dinner on a weekday, but it won't cover the ride home after the late shows, and it doesn't run on weekends. The festival's Plan Your Visit page lists parking, rideshare and cycling options.

Make It a Full Evening

  • Early dinner in Old Town, then walk to the Waterfront as the art lights up at 6 p.m.
  • Catch a fire show early in the evening, before the bridges fill
  • Bring a layer. November evenings cool off quickly once the sun drops

With Kids or Visitors

The event lists family-friendly workshops and activities alongside the art. Grandparents in town for the holidays? The weeknight hours make it an easy, free outing that ends early.
