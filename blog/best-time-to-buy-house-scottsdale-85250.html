Best Time to Buy a House in Scottsdale 85250? October 2026 Read

Best time to buy in Scottsdale 85250? Oct 2026: 3.5 months supply, sales at 97.58% of list. A Chaparral Park agent's Cromford read.

What is the best time to buy a house in Scottsdale? There is no single perfect calendar month. For ZIP 85250 single-family detached homes, the Cromford Report measured October 15, 2026 shows 3.5 months of supply excluding UCB (down from 8.2 last month), sales at 13 per month (up from 5), and average sale price 97.58% of list (versus 100.36% last month) — more negotiating room than a peak frenzy, without signaling a crash.

Searching "best time to buy a house in Scottsdale," "best time to buy house Scottsdale," or "is it a good time to buy a house in Phoenix"? Line up inventory, pricing power and readiness. October supports careful negotiation; waiting for a perfect month is not a strategy by itself.

Buyers often want a calendar answer. What matters more: choice, pricing power, and your readiness. Mid-October 2026 in 85250 leans workable — 3.5 months of supply excl. How to Read October Timing as a Buyer

Choice without overflow. 49 active listings (45 excl. UCB; vs. 46 / 41 LM; 53 / 48 LY) and 3.5 months of supply excl. UCB (103 days of inventory) mean options — not an endless shelf. Last month's 8.2 months of supply swung when sales were only 5/month; pace is back to 13.

Negotiate from comps — do not bank on a crash. Sale-to-list 97.58% (from 100.36%) and median days to first contract 58 (from 36) mean more time and modest concessions. Annual median price is still $870,000 (from $800,000 LY), with 8.8% annual median price appreciation and 4.2% annual $/SF appreciation.

Pace vs. intensity. Listing success 68.4% (from 45.5%); contract ratio 26.7 (from 34.1). My read: more listings find buyers as sales recover, while new-contract intensity vs. inventory is cooler than last month. Move when priced to recent sales; stay patient when not.

  • Watch days to contract. Past the 58-day median to first contract may mean more flexibility; a home priced to recent sales may not wait.
  • Separate ZIP from citywide. Citywide pending is down sharply YoY; 85250 still shows 13 sales/month and 3.5 months of supply excl. UCB.
