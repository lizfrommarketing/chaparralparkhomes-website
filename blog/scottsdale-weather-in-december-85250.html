Scottsdale Weather in December: Average Highs, Lows, Rain and Freeze Odds for 85250

December in Scottsdale averages a 65.6°F high and 43.3°F low at Scottsdale Airport (2001-2025). Rain, freeze odds, what to wear and how to prep an 85250 home.

What's the weather like in Scottsdale in December? Mild days and cool nights. At Scottsdale Airport, December highs averaged 65.6°F and lows 43.3°F from 2001 through 2025, based on NOAA daily records. Rain is light, averaging just under an inch for the month. Freezing nights happen, but rarely: about one per December on average.

For 85250 homeowners, that means patio season by day, a jacket by night and a short list of freeze-prep chores. Here's the detail, plus what this year's El Niño outlook could change.

Data note: Scottsdale figures are my calculations from NOAA daily observations at Scottsdale Municipal Airport (station KSDL) for every December from 2001 through 2025, retrieved through NOAA's Regional Climate Centers ACIS system. Phoenix figures are NOAA's official 1991-2020 normals for Phoenix Sky Harbor. The outlook is NOAA's Oct. 15, 2026 Winter Outlook. Read November 11, 2026. Chaparral Park sits south of the airport, so readings can differ a few degrees.

December Averages at a Glance

Scottsdale Airport, Decembers 2001-2025:

  • Average high: 65.6°F
  • Average low: 43.3°F
  • First week (Dec. 1-7): about 68.6°F / 45.2°F
  • Last week (Dec. 25-31): about 62.5°F / 41.0°F
  • Christmas Day high: averaged 63°F, ranging from 53°F to 75°F
  • Days reaching 70°F or warmer: 32%
  • Days staying below 60°F: 22%

Phoenix Sky Harbor, 1991-2020 normals:

  • Average high / low: 66.2°F / 45.3°F
  • Daily normals: from 69°F / 48°F on Dec. 1 to 66°F / 45°F on Dec. 31
  • Normal precipitation: 0.74 inch

Rain in December

  • Average precipitation (KSDL): 0.98 inch
  • Days with measurable rain: about 4.4 per December
  • Driest Decembers: 0.00 inch in 2005 and 2024
  • Wettest December: 2.29 inches in 2021

This year's wild card: El Niño

NOAA's 2026-27 Winter Outlook favors wetter-than-average conditions across the Southwest from December through February, with a very strong El Niño in place. That tilts the odds; it doesn't guarantee rain. Our Arizona winter forecast 2026 post covers what it could mean.

Freeze Odds

  • Nights at or below 32°F: about 1.1 per December on average
  • Decembers with at least one freeze: 16 of 25
  • Coldest December reading: 28°F on Dec. 29, 2003
  • Warmest December reading: 83°F on Dec. 1, 2021

Sky Harbor, in the urban core, freezes far less often: NOAA records show just 4 December nights at or below 32°F there from 1996 through 2025. Low spots and open yards in 85250 can run colder than the airport sensor on a clear, calm night.

Prep Your 85250 Home for December

Before the first cold night

  • Cover frost-tender plants like bougainvillea and citrus; our frost protection guide has the details
  • Wrap exposed pipes and backflow devices; see do pipes freeze in Arizona?
  • Know your pool equipment's freeze-protection setting

Before the rain

  • Clear roof scuppers, gutters and yard drains
  • Check that sprinklers are on a winter schedule
  • Look for low spots that pond near the foundation

Comfort and energy

  • Test the heat before a cold snap
  • Replace the HVAC filter after fall dust
  • Schedule fireplace or chimney service before you light the first fire

What to Wear and Do

  • Daytime: light layers; many afternoons are T-shirt weather
  • Evenings: a real jacket, especially for outdoor events and holiday lights
  • Snow? Not in town. See does Scottsdale get snow? for the rare exceptions
  • Holiday plans: Scottsdazzle 2026 and the city's lights and markets are all built around these cool, clear evenings

Frequently Asked Questions

How cold does it get in Scottsdale in December?

Lows average 43.3°F at Scottsdale Airport. The coldest December reading since 2001 was 28°F.

What is the average temperature in Scottsdale in December?

About 65.6°F for the high and 43.3°F for the low (2001-2025).

Does it rain in Scottsdale in December?

Lightly: about 0.98 inch over roughly four days, on average.

Is December a good time to visit Scottsdale?

For mild weather, yes. About a third of December days hit 70°F or warmer.
