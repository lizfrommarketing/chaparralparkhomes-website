Tempe Festival of the Arts 2026: Dates, Hours, Parking and Light Rail Tips for the Dec. 4-6 Fall Festival

Tempe Festival of the Arts runs Dec. 4-6, 2026 on Mill Avenue: free, dog friendly, 350-plus artists. Hours, light rail park-and-rides and Kids Block details.

When is the Tempe Festival of the Arts 2026? The 58th fall festival runs Friday-Sunday, Dec. 4-6, 2026, on Mill Avenue in downtown Tempe. Hours are 10 a.m.-5 p.m. Friday and Saturday and 10 a.m.-4 p.m. Sunday. Admission is free, the event is dog friendly, and organizers say more than 350 artists line the streets.

For 85250 neighbors: it's one of the easiest big weekends of the season to reach from south Scottsdale. Take light rail from a park-and-ride and skip downtown parking. Here's the plan.

Data note: Details come from the Tempe Festival of the Arts website (home, festival dates, about, getting here, Kids Block, entertainment, beer and wine, wine and spirits tasting, Chalk-A-Lot and featured artist pages) and Tempe Tourism's holiday events list, read November 10, 2026. Schedules can change; check the festival site before you go.

The Essentials

  • Dates: Fri.-Sun., Dec. 4-6, 2026
  • Hours: 10 a.m.-5 p.m. Fri.-Sat.; 10 a.m.-4 p.m. Sun.
  • Where: Mill Avenue between 3rd Street and University Drive, plus 5th Street between Myrtle and Ash avenues
  • Cost: free admission
  • Artists: as many as 350 booths, chosen by a jury from more than 500 applications across 17 categories
  • Crowds: the spring and fall events each draw nearly 225,000 visitors over three days, per organizers
  • Dogs: welcome on a leash; keep them close so they don't disturb booths
  • Next festival: spring 2027, March 12-14 (tentative)

Getting There From South Scottsdale

Light rail is the easy answer

Organizers call Valley Metro Rail "the most hassle-free way" to the festival, with free parking at 13 park-and-ride lots. Get off at:

  • Mill Ave/3rd St (Station A14), or
  • Tempe Transportation Center (Station A13), one block east of the 5th Street entrance

Park-and-ride lots on the East Valley side include:

  • McClintock Road/Apache Boulevard: 300 spaces
  • Dorsey Lane/Apache Boulevard: 190 spaces
  • Price-101 Freeway/Apache Boulevard: 693 spaces

Streetcar note

During the festival, the Tempe Streetcar runs only between the Dorsey and 9th Street stops.

What to See and Do

The art

Booths cover wood, photography, ceramics, wearable art, jewelry and more. The fall 2026 featured artist is Lana Nguyen, a Phoenix-based mixed-media artist known for hand-colored paper collage on wood. Find her at booths 1145/1147.

Chalk-A-Lot Street

Up to 10 muralists chalk sections of 4th Street west of Mill on Saturday and Sunday. This year's theme is "Hidden Wonders: Discover the Extraordinary." Vote for People's Choice while you're there.

Music

The Centerpoint Stage (Centerpoint Plaza on 7th Street) runs sets all weekend. Saturday features Walt Richardson and Friends at 11:30 a.m., Medio Pinto at 1:30 p.m. and Brea Burns and the Boleros at 3:30 p.m.

Kids Block (Saturday and Sunday)

  • Hands-on art activities, including a LEGO building station and ornament crafting
  • Young Collectors: kids can buy handmade art from a festival artist for $5
  • Young Artist Exhibition: K-12 work from around the Valley, open all three days
  • A Parent's Lounge for breaks, changing and nursing

Food and drinks

Food Truck Alley sits on Maple Avenue between 4th and 5th streets, with a food court near Centerpoint. Three beer and wine locations serve guests 21+ with ID and a wristband. All bars are cashless. The Arizona Wine & Spirits Tasting runs Saturday and Sunday only in Centerpoint Plaza, with wineries including Page Springs Cellars and Arizona Stronghold Vineyards.

“This is one of my favorite December traditions, and it's a real perk of living in south Scottsdale. You can be on Mill Avenue in no time without fighting for parking. My tip: go Sunday morning when it opens, grab a coffee and shop the booths before the afternoon crowd. It's a great place to find a one-of-a-kind housewarming gift.”
— Elizabeth Vijan, Tackett Team, eXp Realty

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Tempe Festival of the Arts 2026?

Dec. 4-6, 2026: 10 a.m.-5 p.m. Friday and Saturday, 10 a.m.-4 p.m. Sunday.

Is the Tempe Festival of the Arts free?

Yes. Admission is free; food, drinks, art and the tasting cost extra.

Where do you park for the Tempe Festival of the Arts?

Use a light rail park-and-ride, such as McClintock/Apache or Dorsey/Apache, and ride to Mill Ave/3rd St.

What is there for kids at the Tempe Festival of the Arts?

The Kids Block (Saturday and Sunday), $5 Young Collectors art and the Young Artist Exhibition.
