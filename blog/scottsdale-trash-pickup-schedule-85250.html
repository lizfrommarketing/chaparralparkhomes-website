Scottsdale Trash Pickup Schedule for 85250: How to Find Your Day, Holidays and Recycling Rules

What is the Scottsdale trash pickup schedule? Look up your address in My Services. Set bins out by 5 a.m. 2026 holiday delays and recycling rules included.

What is the Scottsdale trash pickup schedule? It is address-specific. The City of Scottsdale Solid Waste My Services page is the official way to see your trash, recycling, and brush and bulk dates. Type your address into the lookup — there is no one pickup day that covers every home in ZIP 85250.

When do the bins go out? City guidelines say to place containers at the curb by 5 a.m. on collection day (or after 6 p.m. the night before), keep them at least four feet from other obstacles, and bring them in the same day.

Data note: All schedule, holiday, rate and recycling rules in this post come from City of Scottsdale Solid Waste pages fetched November 5, 2026. This article focuses on weekly refuse and recycling. For monthly brush and bulk weeks, see our separate Scottsdale bulk trash schedule 2026 post.

How to Find Your 85250 Collection Day

  1. Open the city's My Services address lookup
  2. Type your address using single-letter directions and short street abbreviations (example format the city gives: 8520 E San Miguel Av)
  3. Select your address from the autocomplete drop-down, then enter
  4. Read your trash, recycle, and brush/bulk results

The Trash and Recycling hub repeats the same instruction: schedules are on My Services, not on a one-size-fits-all calendar graphic for the whole city.

Solid Waste customer service: 480-312-5600.

Weekly Refuse Basics

From Scottsdale's trash collection guidelines:

  • Black trash container for bagged garbage
  • Bag and tie garbage and grass before it goes in the black cart — required by law and helps with odors and pests
  • Lid must close (Maricopa County Health Department standards)
  • Four-foot clearance from other containers, cars, mailboxes and obstacles
  • Out by 5 a.m.; remove the same day
  • Do not put rocks, dirt, building materials, electronics or hazardous waste in the trash cart

Current residential rates (city-published)

  • Single-family monthly rate: $32.42 (includes a $0.08 state recycling fee)
  • Additional refuse container: $16.45 per month
  • Extra container purchase: $108.00 refuse / $108.00 recycle (one-time), via Solid Waste at 480-312-5600

Hand-collection routes (where used) have separate container rules — durable rigid cans no larger than 32 gallons, max three cans, max 60 pounds each when full. Confirm your service type in My Services if your street is not automated.

2026 Holiday Collection Changes

Per the city's 2026 Residential Single Family Home Holiday Collection Schedule:

Service NOT affected

  • Martin Luther King Jr. Day — Monday, Jan. 19, 2026
  • Presidents' Day — Monday, Feb. 16, 2026
  • Memorial Day — Monday, May 25, 2026
  • Juneteenth — Friday, June 19, 2026
  • Independence Day — Saturday, July 4, 2026
  • Labor Day — Monday, Sept. 7, 2026
  • Veterans Day — Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026

Service delayed

| Holiday | What changes | | --- | --- | | New Year's Day — Thu, Jan. 1, 2026 | No collection that day; Thursday routes move to Friday; Friday routes move to Saturday | | Thanksgiving — Thu, Nov. 26, 2026 | No collection that day; Thursday → Friday; Friday → Saturday (city offices closed Thu–Fri) | | Christmas Day — Fri, Dec. 25, 2026 | Mon–Thu routes unchanged; no Friday collection; Friday routes move to Saturday |

Always re-check the city's holiday page before a long weekend — and still confirm your regular weekday in My Services.

Recycling Rules (Mauve Bin)

Scottsdale's recycling guidelines are strict about contamination:

Accepted (clean, dry, empty)

  • Plastic #1–7: bottles, jugs, tubs, cups, yogurt containers, clamshells, lids on
  • Paper and cardboard (break down boxes); magazines; newspapers
  • Aseptic milk / juice / soup cartons
  • Aluminum cans and clean foil
  • Metal food and beverage cans
  • Glass food and beverage containers — clear, green or amber

Keep out

  • Plastic bags and liners (top contamination source — take bags to participating grocery drop-offs)
  • Styrofoam, packing peanuts
  • Food or liquid waste; greasy pizza boxes
  • Yard waste, construction debris, diapers, pet waste
  • Electronics, batteries, hazardous containers (motor oil, pool chemicals)
  • Shredded paper (trash it, compost it or use a shredding service)

Extra mauve recycling containers also cost $108.00 one-time from Solid Waste. Public drop-off bins have been permanently removed, per the city page.

Bulk Trash vs. Weekly Refuse

Weekly carts are for bagged household trash and accepted recyclables. Brush and bulk is a separate monthly program by area. If you need sofa, appliance or yard-trimming weeks, use our dedicated guide: Scottsdale bulk trash schedule 2026 for 85250 — and still verify your address in My Services.

Household hazardous waste and electronics also have separate city programs; do not put those materials in the black cart.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Scottsdale trash pickup schedule?

Use My Services with your exact address. Schedules vary across 85250.

What time do I put trash out in Scottsdale?

By 5 a.m. on collection day, or after 6 p.m. the night before. Four-foot clearance; lids closed; bring cans in the same day.

Does Scottsdale trash pickup run on holidays in 2026?

Many holidays keep normal service. New Year's Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas shift Thursday/Friday routes as listed on the city's holiday schedule.

What can I put in Scottsdale recycling?

Clean, dry, empty accepted plastics, paper, cardboard, cartons, cans and food-and-beverage glass. No plastic bags or Styrofoam.
