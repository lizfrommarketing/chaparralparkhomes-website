Hanukkah in Scottsdale 2026: Menorah Lightings at Soleri Plaza, Scottsdale Quarter and Around the Valley Near 85250

Hanukkah 2026 runs Dec. 4-12. Public menorah lightings in Scottsdale at Soleri Plaza on Dec. 10 and Scottsdale Quarter on Dec. 6, plus Carefree, Fountain Hills and Tempe.

When is Hanukkah 2026, and where are Scottsdale's menorah lightings? Hanukkah begins at sundown on Friday, Dec. 4, and ends at nightfall on Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026. Scottsdale has two public menorah lightings on the calendar: Scottsdale Quarter on Sunday, Dec. 6, 5 to 7 p.m., and Soleri Plaza in Old Town on Thursday, Dec. 10, 4:30 to 6 p.m.

Closest to Chaparral Park: the free Soleri Plaza celebration near the Scottsdale Waterfront, part of the city's Scottsdazzle season. For a full eight nights of lightings, Carefree hosts a nightly celebration Dec. 4-11.

Hanukkah 2026 at a Glance

  • First candle: Friday, Dec. 4, before sundown
  • Eighth candle: Friday, Dec. 11, before sundown
  • Final day: Saturday, Dec. 12, ending at nightfall
  • Also spelled: Chanukah; we keep each organizer's spelling below

Scottsdale Menorah Lightings

Public Hanukkah Celebration at Soleri Plaza

  • When: Thursday, Dec. 10, 4:30 to 6 p.m.
  • Where: Soleri Plaza, 4420 N. Scottsdale Road, by the Scottsdale Waterfront
  • Host: Chabad of South Scottsdale
  • What to expect: a larger-than-life menorah, a family-friendly public lighting, music, singing and kids' crafts
  • Cost: free, per Scottsdazzle
  • Parking: Old Town on-street parking has no meters or fees, just time limits

Make it an evening: the free Scottsdazzle ArtWalk runs the same night, 7 to 9 p.m. See our Scottsdazzle 2026 guide.

Grand Menorah Lighting at Scottsdale Quarter

  • When: Sunday, Dec. 6, 5 to 7 p.m.
  • Where: Scottsdale Quarter, 15279 N. Scottsdale Road
  • Host: Chabad of Scottsdale
  • What to expect: a giant menorah lighting, music, giveaways and prizes, Chanukah treats and entertainment for all ages

More from Chabad of Scottsdale

Chabad of Scottsdale's Chanukah events page also lists: - Chanukah LIT!, Sunday, Dec. 6, 11 a.m. to 12:15 p.m., for ages 5-13 - Chanukah Car Parade, Monday, Dec. 7, 5 p.m. - Light Up: North Scottsdale, Thursday, Dec. 10, 5 p.m.

Check the organizer's page for locations and registration.

Around the Valley

Chanukah in Carefree

  • When: nightly Dec. 4-11, 5:30 p.m.
  • Where: Sanderson Lincoln Pavilion, 101 Easy St., Carefree
  • Highlights: the celebration's 10th anniversary, with a different theme each night, including a Phoenix Boys Choir performance on Dec. 6
  • Admission: community celebration; donations welcomed

Chanukah on the Avenue, Fountain Hills

  • When: Sunday, Dec. 6, 4:30 p.m.
  • Where: across from the Chabad Edelman Jewish Center, 16830 E. Avenue of the Fountains
  • Highlights: public menorah lighting, music, latkes, doughnuts, dreidels and chocolate gelt
  • Admission: free

Downtown Tempe Menorah Lighting

  • When: Wednesday, Dec. 9, 5:30 to 7 p.m.
  • Host: presented in partnership with ASU Chabad
  • Admission: free; check the event website for the exact gathering spot

Frying Latkes at Home? A Quick Kitchen Note

Latkes and sufganiyot are fried in oil, and a little planning keeps the kitchen safe and the plumbing clear:

  • Stay in the kitchen while frying, and keep a lid beside the pan. NFPA's advice for a small grease fire: slide the lid over the pan, turn off the burner and never throw water on it
  • Watch for smoke. NFPA calls wisps of smoke a sign the oil is too hot; turn off the burner
  • Never pour used oil down the drain. Scottsdale Water says to let it cool, pour it into a container with something absorbent and put it in the trash. Our guide to disposing of cooking grease has the details

Tips for Attending

  • Arrive early. Outdoor evening events get chilly in December; Raising Arizona Kids suggests bringing jackets
  • Check for registration. Lightings are typically open to the public, but dinners and classes may require tickets or RSVPs
  • Confirm details the day of, since organizers may adjust for weather
