How Much Is My House Worth? 85250 Home Values, Zestimate Accuracy and How a CMA Gets You a Real Number

What's your 85250 home worth right now? October 2026 sold prices, how accurate a Zestimate really is (Zillow's own numbers) and how a CMA turns an estimate into a price.

How much is my house worth? In 85250, the typical single-family home sold for $825,000 in October 2026, and $865,000 is the year-to-date median through October, according to Phoenix REALTORS. Your home's number depends on how it compares with the specific homes that just sold around it, which is exactly what a comparative market analysis (CMA) measures.

What about the online estimate? Zillow's own published figures show a nationwide median error of 7.21% for off-market homes. On an $825,000 house, that's roughly $59,500 in either direction, half the time. A useful starting point, not a price.

Data note: 85250 and Scottsdale figures are from Phoenix REALTORS/ShowingTime Local Market Updates for October 2026 (current as of November 3, 2026, ARMLS data). Zestimate accuracy is from Zillow's Zestimate page (refreshed November 8, 2026). Mortgage rates are from Freddie Mac's PMMS. Read November 19, 2026.

  • Year-to-date median: $865,000 through October (up 6.8%)

Townhouses and condos, October 2026

How to read this: a single month with 13 sales swings easily, so the year-to-date median is the steadier anchor. For context, Scottsdale's citywide single-family median was $1,225,000 in October, so 85250 remains one of the more accessible ways into central Scottsdale.

How Accurate Is a Zestimate?

Zillow is unusually transparent here. From its Zestimate page:

  • Nationwide median error: 1.80% for homes on the market and 7.21% for off-market homes
  • Phoenix metro, on-market homes: 1.39% median error, with 97.48% within 10% of the sale price
  • Why the gap: once a home is listed, the model can use the list price, listing details and recent activity. Off-market homes rely on tax records, prior sales and public data

Zillow also notes that unreported additions, updates and remodels aren't reflected in the Zestimate, and that it is not an appraisal. That matters in 85250, where plenty of older homes have been remodeled inside while their public records still describe the original house.

Three Ways to Answer "What Is My House Worth?"

1. Online estimate (free, instant, broad)

Good for tracking direction over time. Treat it as a range, especially if you've remodeled.

2. Comparative market analysis (free, local, specific)

A CMA is what an agent prepares before recommending a list price. A good one looks at:

  • Sold comps from the last few months, as close to your street as possible
  • Pending sales, which show where buyers are agreeing right now
  • Active competition you would be priced against
  • Adjustments for square footage, pool, lot, garage, updates and condition

For a deeper look at pricing strategy once you have that number, see how to price your house.

3. Appraisal (paid, formal, lender-driven)

A licensed appraiser's opinion of value, usually ordered by the buyer's lender after you're under contract.

What Moves Your Number Up or Down in 85250

  • Updates that show: kitchens, baths, flooring, windows and a newer roof or HVAC
  • Pool condition: a resurfaced, well-equipped pool reads very differently from one that needs work
  • Lot and location: proximity to Chaparral Park, the Indian Bend Wash greenbelt and Old Town
  • Negotiation room: sellers received 96.8% of list price in October, so leave a realistic cushion

Rates matter too. Freddie Mac's PMMS put the 30-year fixed average at 7.11% on November 12, 2026, down from 7.18% the week before, which shapes what buyers can pay.

How accurate is a Zillow Zestimate?

What is a CMA in real estate?

A comparative market analysis: an agent's value estimate built from recent sales, pending deals and active listings, adjusted for your home's features.
