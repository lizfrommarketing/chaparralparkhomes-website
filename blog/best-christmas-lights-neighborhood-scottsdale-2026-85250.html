Best Christmas Lights Neighborhoods in Scottsdale 2026: Winter Wonderland in 85250, Voltaire Avenue and a Self-Guided Lights Loop

The best Scottsdale neighborhood Christmas lights for 2026, starting with Winter Wonderland Arizona in ZIP 85250. Dates, hours, addresses and a self-guided lights loop.

Where are the best Christmas lights in Scottsdale? The headliner is right here in ZIP 85250: Winter Wonderland Arizona at 8414 E. Valley Vista Drive, a walk-through display the family's website lists as open nightly from 6 to 10 p.m., Dec. 5 to 28. After that, plan a loop that takes in the Cox Family Lights, the 55th Street cul-de-sac, Grayhawk's charity street and the music-synced Voltaire Avenue corridor.

What it costs: nothing to look. Phoenix New Times' 2026 roundup lists these home displays as free, though several invite donations or sell cocoa.

Data note: Dates, hours and light counts come from each display's own website where available, Phoenix New Times (Nov. 24, 2026), Experience Scottsdale, Scottsdale Mom (Nov. 21, 2026) and Raising Arizona Kids (Nov. 16, 2026). Sources sometimes disagree; we note where. Read November 26, 2026. Home displays are run by private families and can change without notice.

Winter Wonderland Arizona (85250)

The Valley's most famous home display sits inside our own ZIP code.

  • Address: 8414 E. Valley Vista Dr., Scottsdale 85250
  • Hours: nightly, 6 to 10 p.m., Dec. 5 to 28, per the family's site. Phoenix New Times lists Dec. 4 through Jan. 3, so confirm before you go
  • Scale: Experience Scottsdale puts it at 265,000 lights, with a castle facade on the roof, snow-covered hills, a marshmallow fire pit and a miniature mine
  • Shows: nine computerized shows with snowfall, bubbles and pyrotechnics, per Experience Scottsdale
  • Pedigree: the family's timeline notes a win on The Great Christmas Light Fight (Season 5, Episode 5) in 2017 and a Spirit of Scottsdale award in 2019

Scottsdale Mom's advice: park, bundle up and walk through, and bring cash for hot cocoa and s'mores.

Cox Family Lights (85258)

  • Address: 8607 E. Via Del Palacio, Scottsdale
  • Hours: 6 to 9 p.m. Sunday through Thursday and 6 to 10 p.m. Friday and Saturday, Dec. 1 to 30, per Phoenix New Times
  • Highlights: a 20-foot tree, about 40,000 bulbs, characters like Gus the Christmas Bat, and synchronized 40-minute light shows programmed by Brolin Cox

North Scottsdale and the Phoenix Border

55th Street Cul-de-sac

  • Address: 11802 N. 55th St., Scottsdale 85254
  • What you'll see: four homes decorated together, about 100,000 lights per Experience Scottsdale, and a large white sleigh for photos

Grayhawk's Charity Street

  • Where: N. 74th Way and E. Rose Garden Lane
  • The draw: music, lights and Santa's Cocoa Hut. Experience Scottsdale says neighbors support St. Mary's Food Bank and recently collected more than 30,000 pounds of donations
  • Nearby: the Montevina neighborhood around 20726 N. 76th Way, where Scottsdale Mom and Raising Arizona Kids note a canned-food drive in past years

Voltaire Avenue

  • Where: 4600 to 4800 blocks of E. Voltaire Ave., near Tatum Boulevard and Thunderbird Road
  • Hours: 5:30 to 10 p.m. Monday to Thursday, 5:30 to 11 p.m. Friday to Sunday, through Dec. 31, per Phoenix New Times
  • Soundtrack: more than two dozen homes sync their lights. Scottsdale Mom says to tune to 98.1 FM

Grovers Avenue and 56th Street

  • Address: around 18002 N. 56th St.
  • What you'll see: a themed, animated display with about 25,000 lights, per Scottsdale Mom

Closer to Chaparral Park

Not every holiday night needs a drive north.

  • 16 Spur Circle (85251): Experience Scottsdale describes 18,000 lights, animated characters and holiday music in a small circular community where many neighbors also decorate
  • McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park Holiday Lights: ticketed holiday lights and train rides at 7301 E. Indian Bend Rd., Nov. 27 to Dec. 30
  • Scottsdazzle: Old Town's month of events, starting with the tree lighting on Saturday, Nov. 28

A Simple Lights Loop

  1. Pick a weeknight if you can, and check each display's dates first
  2. Begin in 85250 at Winter Wonderland, then head north to the Cox display
  3. Finish on Voltaire Avenue with the radio on, since it runs latest on weekends
  4. Be a good guest: stay on public streets, dim your headlights, keep driveways clear and keep voices down after the show
