What Is a BINSR in Arizona? How the Buyer's Inspection Notice and Seller's Response Works for 85250 Buyers and Sellers

A BINSR is the Arizona REALTORS form buyers use after inspections to accept the home, cancel, or ask the seller to fix items. Timelines, options and 85250 negotiating context.

What is a BINSR? It's the Buyer's Inspection Notice and Seller's Response, the Arizona REALTORS® form a buyer delivers before the inspection period ends. On it, the buyer accepts the home as is, cancels the contract, or lists items for the seller to correct or address. The seller then has 5 days, unless the contract says otherwise, to answer.

Why it matters: the BINSR is a one-shot document. The form says the buyer must list every disapproved item in that single notice and can't change the election after delivering it. In a market where 85250 sellers received 96.8% of list price in October, it is where most of the real negotiating happens.

Where the BINSR Fits in an Arizona Purchase

  • Contract signed: the inspection period starts. The Phoenix REALTORS® sample contract offers 10 days for residential, or another number the parties choose
  • Inspections and research: home, termite, sewer, pool barrier, insurance quotes and HOA documents
  • BINSR delivered: before the inspection period expires
  • Seller responds: within 5 days or the agreed number
  • Buyer's final choice: cancel or proceed, within 5 days

The Buyer's Three Options

The BINSR gives the buyer exactly three elections:

  1. Premises Accepted: no corrections requested; the buyer takes the home in its present condition
  2. Premises Rejected: the buyer disapproves of the listed items and cancels the contract immediately
  3. Opportunity to correct: the buyer lists disapproved items and gives the seller a chance to correct or address them

"Address" is a deliberate word. A seller can respond with a repair, a credit, a price change, or some mix. The form notes that if the seller agrees to a monetary credit or price change, an addendum must go to the buyer's lender, which may limit total credits.

What the Buyer Acknowledges

By signing, the buyer confirms:

  • All desired inspections are done, including physical, environmental, square footage, wood-destroying insects, sewer, flood hazard, pool barriers and insurance
  • Every disapproved item is in this notice
  • Their election is limited to the options on the form
  • They can't change the election after delivery

The form also covers who does the work. Citing A.R.S. § 32-1121, it says a licensed contractor must perform repairs when the total price is $1,000 or more, the work isn't casual or minor, or it needs a building permit. Agreed repairs must be done in a workmanlike manner.

The Seller's Response

When the buyer offers a chance to correct, the seller checks one of three boxes:

  • Agrees to correct or address the items
  • Is unwilling or unable to correct or address any of them
  • Counters with a specific response, often with an addendum

In the Phoenix REALTORS® sample contract, silence is a no: a seller who doesn't respond in writing on time is deemed to have refused. If the seller agrees to repairs, that contract calls for paid receipts delivered 3 days before close of escrow.

The Buyer's Final Word

If the seller doesn't agree to everything, the buyer decides:

  • Cancel, with earnest money returned, within 5 days of the seller's response or the response deadline, whichever comes first
  • Accept the seller's response and close with only the agreed items

Miss that window and the buyer proceeds with only what the seller agreed to in writing. More on deposits in is earnest money refundable in Arizona.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does BINSR mean in real estate?

Buyer's Inspection Notice and Seller's Response, the Arizona form for post-inspection requests.

How long does a seller have to respond to a BINSR?

Five days, unless the contract states another number.

What happens if the seller refuses the BINSR requests?

The buyer cancels or closes without those items, within 5 days.

Can a buyer change a BINSR after sending it?

No. List everything in the first notice.
