Indian School Park Scottsdale: Tennis Center Hours, Court Fees and Park Amenities

Indian School Park & Tennis Center sits at 4289 N. Hayden Rd., just south of 85250. Here are its 13 courts, winter hours, court fees, drop-in times and park amenities.

What is Indian School Park? It's a City of Scottsdale park and tennis center at 4289 N. Hayden Road, on Hayden between Camelback and Indian School roads, a short drive south of Chaparral Park. It's best known for its 13 rebuilt post-tension concrete tennis courts and a tennis center the USTA Southwest Section named its member organization of the year.

What does it cost to play? Court fees are $3 per person for Scottsdale residents before 6 p.m. ($5 non-residents), and $5 for residents from 6 to 9 p.m. ($8 non-residents). The park is open sunrise to 10:30 p.m.

Data note: Addresses, hours, fees and amenities come from City of Scottsdale park and tennis pages (updated May–August 2026) and the city's park map. Confirm schedules with the park before you go. No market statistics are included in this post.

Indian School Park at a Glance

  • Address: 4289 N. Hayden Rd., Scottsdale, AZ 85251
  • Phone: 480-312-2740
  • Park hours: sunrise to 10:30 p.m.
  • Tennis courts: 13, rebuilt post-tension concrete, with blended lines for junior play
  • Programs: leagues, Cardio Tennis, lessons, ball machine rentals, tournaments, drop-in doubles
  • Juniors: Net Generation program for ages 7 to 12

Tennis Center Hours by Season

Fall (Labor Day to the Sunday After Thanksgiving)

  • Mon.–Fri.: 7:30 a.m. to 9 p.m.
  • Sat.–Sun.: 7:30 a.m. to 7 p.m.

Winter (Monday After Thanksgiving to the Day Before Presidents Day)

  • Mon.–Fri.: 9 a.m. to 9 p.m.
  • Sat.–Sun.: 9 a.m. to 7 p.m.

Spring (Presidents Day to Memorial Day)

Same as fall: 7:30 a.m. openings.

Summer (After Memorial Day to the Day Before Labor Day)

  • Mon.–Fri.: 6:30 to 10:30 a.m. and 5:30 to 9 p.m.
  • Sat.–Sun.: 6:30 to 10:30 a.m.

With Thanksgiving on Nov. 26 this year, the later winter openings start Monday, Nov. 30, 2026.

Court Fees, Reservations and Drop-In Play

Fees

  • Before 6 p.m.: $3 residents, $5 non-residents, per person
  • 6 to 9 p.m.: $5 residents, $8 non-residents, per person
  • Ball machine, before 6 p.m.: $11 for 1 hour, $13 for 1.5, $15 for 2
  • Ball machine, 6 to 9 p.m.: $15, $17 and $19
  • Junior rates are available

Reservations

Call 480-312-2740. The city takes court reservations by phone, one day ahead, starting at 9:30 a.m., for 90-minute slots. You reserve a time, not a specific court.

Drop-In Mixed Doubles

Tuesdays and Thursdays, $3 per person, no partner or registration needed. Start times are 7:30 a.m. in fall and spring, 9 a.m. in winter and 7 a.m. in summer. Bring your own balls, arrive 15 minutes early, and call ahead to confirm.

If the courts are booked, the city's other tennis center is Scottsdale Ranch Park & Tennis Center at 10400 E. Via Linda (480-312-7774), which uses the same phone-in reservation rules.

Beyond Tennis

The city lists these features:

  • Ball field, plus a map showing Fields 1 through 4
  • Bocce, cornhole, horseshoe pits and shuffleboard
  • Volleyball and racquetball
  • A lake, a playground, ramadas and restrooms

The map also places the Indian Bend Visitor Center inside the park. Ramadas book through the Facility Booking Office at 480-312-7707; sports field requests need at least three business days' notice.
