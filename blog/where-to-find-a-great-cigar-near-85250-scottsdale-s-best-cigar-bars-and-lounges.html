Where to Find a Great Cigar Near 85250: Scottsdale's Best Cigar Bars and Lounges

Scottsdale's best cigar bars and lounges, and how close they are to Chaparral Park and 85250.

It's not the first amenity most buyers ask about, but it comes up more than you'd think — especially from clients relocating from cities where indoor cigar lounges are hard to find or heavily restricted. Scottsdale has a genuinely strong cigar bar scene, and several of the best options are a short drive from the 85250 zip code.

Here's the rundown.

Churchill's Fine Cigars: The Local Standard

Churchill's has built a reputation as one of the best cigar lounges in the Valley, and it's not hard to see why. Locally owned since 1995, Churchill's Scottsdale location features the largest humidor in Scottsdale and is the only craft-cocktail smoking lounge in the city, meaning you can enjoy a well-made cocktail and a cigar indoors in the same upscale setting.

As Davidoff Appointed Merchants and active members of the Tobacconists' Association of America, Churchill's stocks top-tier brands like Padron, Arturo Fuente, and My Father, alongside an exclusive selection of TAA-only cigars. The bourbon program is equally serious, with more than 80 different bourbons on hand, including hard-to-find bottles like Pappy Van Winkle, plus a deep selection of single-malt Scotches.

The atmosphere is intentionally low-key and polished rather than loud — Churchill's pitches itself as a place for a relaxed cigar and conversation, or for hosting clients in a professional but comfortable setting. The heated and misted patio extends the experience outdoors during Scottsdale's milder months.

Churchill's North Scottsdale location sits at the junction of the 101 and Scottsdale Road, making it a quick and easy drive from most of the 85250 area.

Fox Cigar Bar: Old Town's Open-Concept Lounge

Fox Cigar Bar's Old Town Scottsdale location offers a different vibe — a more open, social design with custom leather chairs, high-top and bar seating, and a lively outdoor patio. With 34 TVs covering every major sport, it leans more toward a social, watch-the-game-with-a-good-stick atmosphere than Churchill's quieter lounge feel.

The cocktail and spirits program is well-regarded, and the humidor carries a solid rotating selection. For 85250 residents who want to pair a cigar bar visit with a broader night out in Old Town's restaurant and bar scene, Fox Cigar Bar's location makes that easy.

Cedar Room: North Scottsdale's Members-Focused Lounge

Cedar Room, located in the DC Ranch area of North Scottsdale, takes a more exclusive approach with humidified, temperature-controlled private cigar lockers available to members, alongside a climate-controlled lounge and patio that operates year-round. As a Davidoff Appointed Merchant, Cedar Room carries limited releases that aren't always available elsewhere in the Valley.

It's a longer drive from 85250 than Churchill's or Fox, but it's worth knowing about for buyers who want a more private, club-like cigar experience and don't mind the extra distance.

Why This Matters for 85250 Buyers

I'll be honest: a strong cigar bar scene isn't going to be the deciding factor for most buyers. But for clients who do care about it — and a meaningful number of my move-up and luxury buyers do — it's one more data point in the larger picture of what daily life in 85250 actually looks like. You're close enough to Old Town and North Scottsdale to have real options, without having to live directly inside the noise of either.
