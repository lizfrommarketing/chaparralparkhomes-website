Is McCormick Ranch a Good Place to Live? Lakes, Greenbelt, Prices and How It Compares With Chaparral Park 85250

McCormick Ranch, Scottsdale's first master-planned community, sits just north of Chaparral Park. Lakes, greenbelt paths, October 2026 prices for 85258 vs. 85250, and how to choose.

Is McCormick Ranch a good place to live? For many Scottsdale buyers, yes: it is a mature, master-planned community of about 3,116 acres with 11 lakes, roughly 25 miles of greenbelt and pathways, two golf courses and shopping built into the plan. It starts just across Indian Bend Road from Chaparral Park, so the two areas share the same Indian Bend Wash greenbelt and the same central location.

What it costs right now: McCormick Ranch sits mainly in ZIP 85258, where the October 2026 single-family median was $1,235,000, according to Phoenix REALTORS. Next door in 85250, it was $825,000. The gap is real, and it is the main thing to weigh.

Data note: Market figures are from Phoenix REALTORS Local Market Updates for 85258 and 85250, October 2026. ZIP 85258 also includes areas outside McCormick Ranch, so treat it as a proxy. Community facts are from the McCormick Ranch Property Owners' Association and Scottsdale Progress (Sept. 2022). Rents are Zillow ZORI, October 2026. Read November 24, 2026.

McCormick Ranch at a Glance

  • Size: about 3,116 acres, roughly seven square miles
  • Boundaries: Indian Bend Road north to Shea Boulevard, Scottsdale Road east to Hayden Road (per Scottsdale Progress)
  • Lakes: 11 lakes on about 120 acres, per the MRPOA land-use table
  • Golf: two golf courses on about 303 acres
  • Paths: about 25 miles of greenbelt and pathways
  • Built in: shopping centers, places of worship, two resort hotels, a medical center and a post office, according to the MRPOA
  • History: the former McCormick family ranch; master developer Kaiser-Aetna bought it in 1969 and began building homes in the early 1970s

What Daily Life Looks Like

Water, paths and parks

The lakes and greenbelts are the signature. You can walk or bike long stretches on paths instead of streets. McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park sits near Scottsdale and Indian Bend roads, and the 30-acre Mountain View Park recreation center is inside the community.

Shopping and services

The plan mixes in neighborhood centers, the HonorHealth campus, Scottsdale's main police station and Mustang Library, so errands stay close.

Homes

You'll find single-family homes, condos and apartments across dozens of subdivisions. Lakefront and golf-course lots carry a premium, and each subdivision can have its own design rules.

McCormick Ranch (85258) vs. Chaparral Park (85250): October 2026

Single-family homes, Phoenix REALTORS:

  • Median price, October: $1,235,000 in 85258 (down 8.1%) vs. $825,000 in 85250 (down 7.2%)
  • Median price, year to date: $1,325,000 (up 2.9%) vs. $865,000 (up 6.8%)
  • Closed sales, October: 36 vs. 13
  • Days on market: 76 vs. 89
  • Months of supply: 3.1 vs. 4.2
  • Share of list price received: 96.8% in both

Typical rent (Zillow ZORI, Oct. 2026): about $2,248 in 85258, up 5.2% from a year ago, and $2,503 in 85250, up 3.6%.

What that tells you: 85258 has a higher price point, more October closings and tighter supply. In 85250, buyers have a bit more inventory to choose from, and median prices are lower.

Questions to Ask Before You Buy in McCormick Ranch

  1. Which associations apply? The master MRPOA plus any sub-HOA. Get dues, reserves and rules for both.
  2. Lakefront? Ask who maintains the retaining wall and what the CC&Rs require.
  3. Rental rules? Check both sets of documents before you count on rental income.
  4. Age of systems? Much of the community was built from the 1970s on, so roof, AC and plumbing age matter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is McCormick Ranch a good place to live?

Many owners value its lakes, 25 miles of paths and built-in shopping. Tour several subdivisions to see which fits you.

Where is McCormick Ranch in Scottsdale?

Indian Bend Road north to Shea Boulevard, between Scottsdale and Hayden roads, just north of Chaparral Park.

What do homes cost in McCormick Ranch?

In ZIP 85258, the October 2026 single-family median was $1,235,000; year to date, $1,325,000.

Does McCormick Ranch have an HOA?

Yes, the MRPOA, and many subdivisions add their own association.
