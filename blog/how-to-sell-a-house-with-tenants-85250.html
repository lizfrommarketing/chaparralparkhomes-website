How to Sell a House With Tenants Living in It: Arizona Notice Rules, Your Four Options and What 85250 Buyers Expect

Selling an 85250 rental with tenants in place? Arizona's two-day showing notice and 30-day month-to-month rule, four ways to sell, and October 2026 rent and sales data.

Can you sell a house with tenants in it? Yes. In Arizona you can sell a rental with the tenant in place, and the lease generally stays in force, so your buyer steps in as the new landlord. Your real choice is who you're selling to: an investor who wants the income, or an owner-occupant who needs the home empty, which means timing the sale around the lease.

What does the law require? Arizona's Residential Landlord and Tenant Act says tenants can't unreasonably refuse showings to prospective buyers, and landlords must give at least two days' notice before entering, at reasonable times.

The Rules That Shape Every Tenant-Occupied Sale

Showings and access (A.R.S. 33-1343)

  • Tenants shall not unreasonably withhold consent for the landlord to show the home to prospective or actual purchasers
  • The landlord must give at least two days' notice and enter only at reasonable times, except in emergencies
  • The landlord may not abuse access or use it to harass the tenant

Ending a month-to-month tenancy (A.R.S. 33-1375)

  • Either side may end a month-to-month tenancy with written notice at least 30 days before the periodic rental date named in the notice
  • A fixed-term lease generally runs to its end date unless you and the tenant agree otherwise in writing

Security deposits (A.R.S. 33-1321)

  • At the end of a tenancy, after the tenant moves out and asks, the landlord must provide an itemized list of deductions and any refund within 14 days, excluding weekends and legal holidays
  • If the tenant stays and the buyer takes over, make sure the deposit and lease are transferred and documented at closing

This is general information, not legal advice. Have a real estate attorney review your lease before you list.

Your Four Options

1. Sell to an investor, tenant in place

Best when the tenant pays on time and the lease runs months into 2027. Market the home on its income. Investors will want the lease, rent roll, deposit amount and maintenance history.

2. Wait for the lease to end, then sell vacant

Best for top dollar from owner-occupants. A vacant home can be repaired, cleaned and staged, and shown on your schedule. The trade-off is carrying costs with no rent.

3. Negotiate an early move-out

Some landlords offer a tenant a written mutual agreement to end the lease early, often with moving help or a rent credit. Put any agreement in writing with clear dates.

4. Offer the tenant the first chance to buy

Your tenant already knows the house. Even if they don't buy, asking keeps the relationship cooperative, and cooperation is what makes showings work.

With homes taking close to three months to sell and condo supply rising, a cooperative tenant and a clean, well-shown home matter more than they did in a faster market.

Making Showings Work With a Tenant in Place

  • Talk first, in person. Explain the timeline before the sign goes up
  • Set showing windows in writing, with the required two days' notice
  • Offer a thank-you: a rent credit or cleaning service for showing weeks is common courtesy
  • Use great photos and a video tour to cut down on unnecessary walk-throughs
  • Keep up repairs. Fix what the tenant reports; it shows in photos and inspections

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I sell my house with tenants living in it in Arizona?

Yes. The lease generally continues, and the buyer becomes the landlord, unless the lease ends or you and the tenant agree otherwise.

How much notice do I have to give a tenant to show the house in Arizona?

At least two days, at reasonable times, under A.R.S. 33-1343.

How much notice to end a month-to-month lease in Arizona?

Written notice at least 30 days before the periodic rental date, under A.R.S. 33-1375.

Is it harder to sell a house with tenants?

It can be, but a strong lease can attract investors, and good communication keeps showings smooth.
