How to Clean a Dishwasher After the Holidays: The Filter, a Vinegar Rinse and Hard-Water Fixes for 85250 Kitchens

How to clean a dishwasher: rinse the filter, run a cleaner or vinegar cycle, and wipe the gasket. Why Scottsdale's very hard water means 85250 dishwashers need it more often.

How do you clean a dishwasher? Pull and rinse the filter, wipe the door gasket and spray arms, then run an empty hot cycle with a dishwasher cleaner tablet or a cup of white vinegar. Whirlpool recommends doing it about once every 30 days, and more often if you run the machine daily.

Why it matters more in 85250: Scottsdale's water is very hard, roughly 13 to 17 grains per gallon by the city's 2026 report. That mineral load is what leaves white film on glasses and scale inside the machine. After a week of holiday dishes, this is the right weekend to do it.

Data note: Cleaning steps and frequency come from Whirlpool's and GE Appliances' published dishwasher care guides. Hardness figures come from the City of Scottsdale's 2026 Water Quality Report and the U.S. Geological Survey's hardness scale. Read November 27, 2026. Always follow your own owner's manual.

What You'll Need

  • A soft sponge or cloth and warm, soapy water
  • A small soft brush, such as an old toothbrush
  • A dishwasher cleaner tablet, or distilled white vinegar
  • Baking soda (optional, for odors)
  • A towel

Skip: wire brushes and scouring pads. Both GE and Whirlpool warn they can damage the filters.

Step 1: Clean the Filter

The filter is the single biggest fix for smells, gritty dishes and poor cleaning.

  1. Remove the bottom rack.
  2. Find the filter at the bottom of the tub, usually a round cylinder with a flat mesh plate around it.
  3. Twist and lift. On many Whirlpool models, the upper filter assembly turns a quarter turn counterclockwise. On GE models with an Ultra Fine filter, twist it counterclockwise and lift.
  4. Rinse under warm running water. Use a soft brush for stuck-on food or calcium deposits from hard water.
  5. Reinstall and lock it. Whirlpool cautions not to run the dishwasher without the filters in place. If the upper assembly spins freely, it isn't locked.

Note: Some older and some current models use a self-cleaning filter with nothing to remove. Your manual will tell you.

How often: - Whirlpool: monthly, or every two weeks with daily use - GE Appliances: every other month or more, depending on use; sooner if dishes feel gritty

Step 2: Wipe the Gasket, Door and Spray Arms

  • Door gasket: wipe the rubber seal and the bottom edge of the door, where drips collect
  • Spray arms: check the holes for food or scale; rinse the arms if your model lets you remove them
  • Interior walls: a damp cloth handles most residue

Step 3: Run a Cleaning Cycle

With a dishwasher cleaner

Whirlpool's affresh instructions: - Empty machine: put the tablet in the detergent dispenser and run a Normal cycle - With dishes: place the tablet on the bottom of the tub and add detergent as usual - Heavy buildup: run Normal empty, with a tablet in the dispenser and one on the bottom

With vinegar

If white film remains, Whirlpool suggests placing 2 cups of white vinegar in a dishwasher-safe cup on the bottom rack and running a complete cycle using air-dry or energy-saving dry. Use distilled white vinegar to help prevent staining.

Why 85250 Dishwashers Scale Up Faster

The City of Scottsdale publishes hardness by area, with Chaparral Road as one of the dividing lines:

  • Indian School Road to Chaparral Road: about 13-17 grains per gallon
  • North of Chaparral Road: about 13-17 grains per gallon
  • USGS scale: anything over 180 mg/L is "very hard," and every Scottsdale zone lands there

Hard water isn't a health issue, but it shows up as white film, spotty glasses and scale. For a full breakdown, see does Scottsdale have hard water?

Common Dishwasher Problems and First Fixes

  • Smells: clean the filter, wipe the gasket, then run a cleaner cycle
  • Gritty dishes: a clogged filter is the usual cause
  • Standing water after a cycle: Whirlpool says that points to heavy debris near the drain; clean the filter area, then run Normal empty
  • White film: a vinegar rinse or descaling cleaner
  • "Clean Filter" light: on Whirlpool models with one, clean the filter, hold the button for three seconds, then open and close the door

If you also have a garbage disposal on the same drain, a clog there can back water into the dishwasher. See how to reset a garbage disposal.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should you clean a dishwasher?

About every 30 days, per Whirlpool. Check your manual.

How do you clean a dishwasher with vinegar?

Set 2 cups of white vinegar upright on the bottom rack and run a full cycle.

Why does my dishwasher smell?

Usually a dirty filter. Clean it and wipe the gasket.

How do I get white film off dishes in Scottsdale?

It's hard-water scale. A vinegar rinse or descaling cleaner helps.
