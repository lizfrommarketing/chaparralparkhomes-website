Scottsdazzle 2026: Old Town Scottsdale Holiday Dates, Tree Lighting and Events Near 85250

Scottsdazzle 2026 runs Nov. 28 to Dec. 31 in Old Town Scottsdale, opening with a free tree lighting Nov. 28 at 7 p.m. Dates, venues, costs and parking for 85250.

When is Scottsdazzle 2026? The City of Scottsdale's holiday celebration runs November 28 through December 31, 2026, across Old Town. It opens with the free Tree Lighting Spectacular on Saturday, November 28, from 7 to 9 p.m. on the Scottsdale Waterfront Canal Bank, where the city lights a 36-foot tree and marks Scottsdale's 75th anniversary.

Why it matters in 85250: Chaparral Park sits a short drive north of Old Town, so Scottsdazzle is the easiest big holiday outing on the calendar for this ZIP: free parking, mostly free events, and four Saturday evenings you can plan around.

Data note: Dates, times, venues and prices below come from the official Scottsdazzle event pages published by City of Scottsdale Tourism & Events, checked November 9, 2026. Confirm before you go; city events can change.

Scottsdazzle 2026 at a Glance

  • Dates: Nov. 28 – Dec. 31, 2026
  • Kickoff: Tree Lighting Spectacular, Sat. Nov. 28, 7–9 p.m., Scottsdale Waterfront Canal Bank (7135 E. Camelback Rd.), free
  • Activity centers: Scottsdale Waterfront, 5th Avenue, the Arts District and the Civic Center
  • Cost: most signature events are free; a few, like Pancakes & Pajamas with Santa, are ticketed
  • Parking: free in Old Town; on-street spaces have time limits, not meters
  • Organizer: City of Scottsdale Tourism & Events, 480-312-7177

The Tree Lighting Spectacular (Nov. 28)

The official listing describes the kickoff as a community gathering hosted by Arizona Family's Jamie Ceretta, with:

  • Live music from the 10-piece band Phat Cat Swinger
  • Toyland, a hands-on play area for young children
  • Treats from Old Town vendors and a pop-up market hosted by The Merchantile Market
  • Photos with Santa and Mrs. Claus
  • The lighting of the 36-foot Scottsdazzle tree, with 75th-anniversary surprises

The city's own tip: book dinner at a nearby Old Town restaurant first, then walk over.

Key Scottsdazzle Dates to Save

Saturdays on the canal

  • Winter at the Waterfront (Dec. 5 listing): 6–9 p.m. on the Scottsdale Waterfront Canal Bank, free. Holiday lights, live music, hot cocoa, seasonal snacks and pop-up boutiques along Stetson Plaza.
  • Starlight Movie Night: Sat. Dec. 5, 7–9 p.m., Civic Center East Bowl, free. The film is Elf. Tickets aren't required, but the city asks you to register.

Family mornings

  • Meet Santa at the Old Town Scottsdale Farmers Market: Nov. 28 (with more dates in December), photos 9 a.m.–noon. The market runs 8 a.m.–1 p.m. at 3939 N. Drinkwater Blvd., and kids and pets can both get a printed photo.
  • Pancakes & Pajamas with Santa: Sat. Dec. 12, 9–10:30 a.m., Marshall Way Bridge. $30 per person, every attendee needs a paid seat, and tickets are nonrefundable.

Evenings in the Arts District and beyond

  • Gold Palette ArtWalk: Thu. Dec. 10, 7–9 p.m., free, a holiday-themed Scottsdale Gallery Association ArtWalk on Main Street.
  • Public Hanukkah Celebration: Thu. Dec. 10, 4:30–6 p.m., Soleri Plaza, free, a menorah lighting with the Chabad of South Scottsdale.
  • Holiday Dueling Pianos on the Bridge: Sat. Dec. 19, 1–4 p.m., Marshall Way Bridge, free.
  • Live Nativity and Choir Concert: Sat. Dec. 19, 5–8 p.m., Old Adobe Mission, 3817 N. Brown Ave., free.

Shop local, get a souvenir

Scottsdale 75th Shopper Appreciation Days: spend $75 at Old Town businesses between Nov. 28 and Dec. 19, then bring your receipts to an Old Town Information Cart for a commemorative souvenir.

Planning a Scottsdazzle Night From Chaparral Park

  • Go early on Nov. 28. The canal bank fills up for the lighting. Have dinner in Old Town and walk over.
  • Pair it with the market. On Saturday mornings, the Farmers Market and Santa photos make an easy first stop. Our Old Town Scottsdale Farmers Market guide has the details.
  • Layer for the evening. Evenings drop off quickly after sunset in late fall, so bring a jacket for the canal.
  • Mind time limits. Parking is free, but on-street spots are time-limited. For longer evenings, use the free garages on the city's Old Town Parking Map.
  • Make it a lights tour. Combine Scottsdazzle with McCormick-Stillman Railroad Park's holiday lights for a two-night local circuit.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Scottsdazzle 2026?

November 28 through December 31, 2026, opening with the free Tree Lighting Spectacular on November 28 from 7 to 9 p.m.

Where is the Scottsdale Christmas tree lighting?

On the Scottsdale Waterfront Canal Bank, listed at 7135 E. Camelback Road, where the 36-foot Scottsdazzle tree is lit.

Is Scottsdazzle free?

Most signature events are free. Some are ticketed, such as Pancakes & Pajamas with Santa at $30 per person.

Where do you park for Scottsdazzle?

Old Town parking is free. On-street spaces have time limits, and the city's Old Town Parking Map shows free public garages and lots.
