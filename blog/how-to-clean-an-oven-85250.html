How to Clean an Oven After Thanksgiving: The Self-Clean Cycle, the Baking Soda Method and Safety Tips for 85250 Kitchens

How to clean an oven after Thanksgiving: run self-clean the right way, or use baking soda and vinegar. Racks, door glass, smoke and safety tips for 85250 homeowners.

How do you clean an oven after Thanksgiving? Let it cool, take everything out, wipe up loose grease, then either run the self-clean cycle with the kitchen ventilated or spread a baking soda paste, spray it with vinegar and wipe it away. Clean the door frame and racks by hand either way, and skip harsh chemical cleaners in a self-cleaning oven unless your manual allows them.

Why today matters: the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) says Thanksgiving is the peak day of the year for U.S. home cooking fires, with an estimated 1,139 reported on Thanksgiving 2024, more than three times the daily average. A clean oven is a safer one.

Data note: Fire figures are from NFPA (news release, Nov. 12, 2026). Cleaning steps are from GE Appliances' self-clean guidance and Consumer Reports' oven-cleaning articles, which quote engineers at Whirlpool, Maytag and LG. Read November 26, 2026. Always follow your oven's manual.

Before You Start

  • Let it cool completely. Consumer Reports' experts say to wait until the inside is safe to touch
  • Empty it. GE says to remove probes, foil, silicone mats and stored pans. Foil or mats left in during self-clean can damage the oven
  • Check the racks. Dark, enameled racks labeled "Self-Clean" or "Never-Scrub" can stay in. Shiny or silver racks should come out, or they may discolor and warp
  • Wipe loose spills. GE says liquid grease or juices that aren't baked on should be wiped out with a wet cloth first

Option 1: The Self-Clean Cycle

High-heat self-clean is still the most effective built-in method, according to Consumer Reports' oven tester.

  • How it works: the cycle runs 90 minutes to 6 hours at 800°F or higher. The door locks, and food soil burns to ash
  • Clean the frame first: wipe about 1.5 inches around the oven opening by hand, since that area doesn't get hot enough. Keep water out of the door-frame slots and don't scrub or move the door gasket
  • No cleaners: GE says not to use cleaning products on the door, window or interior when prepping
  • Ventilate: open windows or run the hood fan. The first hour or two is the smokiest
  • Protect the household: Consumer Reports warns that pet birds can be very sensitive to the fumes and should be moved to a well-ventilated room. Keep kids away from the hot exterior
  • Wait it out: GE says cooling takes 30 to 90 minutes, and the cooling fan may keep running
  • Finish: wipe the white ash away with water and vinegar on a sponge

Timing tip: Consumer Reports notes the oven is out of commission for hours and the house may smell of burning grime. Don't run it the morning guests arrive. In a double oven, GE says you can't self-clean both cavities at once or bake in one while the other cleans.

Option 2: Baking Soda and Vinegar (No Oven Cleaner)

For lighter messes, or ovens without self-clean:

  1. Make a paste of baking soda and water and spread it over the cool oven interior
  2. Let it sit to loosen the grime
  3. Spray with white vinegar or lemon juice
  4. Wipe with a wet rag, then rinse and dry

That method comes from Whirlpool's kitchen brand manager, quoted by Consumer Reports. For stuck-on spots, cover them with a wet, soapy dishcloth for 10 to 20 minutes before wiping.

Racks, Door Glass and Odors

  • Racks: soak them in dish soap for at least 2 hours or overnight, then scrub with a stiff brush. Too big for the sink? Use the bathtub, lined with towels to protect the finish
  • Door glass: use a damp, not soaking, cloth. Consumer Reports warns that drips can streak between the panes, which may mean taking the door apart
  • Lingering smells: heat the oven on low for about an hour with a baking dish of water and lemon juice, white vinegar or vanilla

How Often Should You Clean an Oven?

Whirlpool's brand manager recommends a thorough cleaning every three to six months, and Consumer Reports' former-chef writer suggests monthly if you often roast fatty meats. Wipe spills once the oven cools, before they bake on.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a self-cleaning oven take?

From 90 minutes to 6 hours, plus 30 to 90 minutes to cool.

Can I use oven cleaner in a self-cleaning oven?

Check the manual first. Chemical cleaners can damage the enamel.

How do you clean an oven with baking soda and vinegar?

Spread a baking soda paste, spray with vinegar or lemon juice, then wipe.

Why is my self-cleaning oven smoking?

Some smoke is normal. Heavy smoke usually means excess grease or an item left inside.
